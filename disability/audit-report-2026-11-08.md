# Warren County, PA — Disability Services Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; also Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon).
**Category:** Disability services.
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Method:** Pilot list of 28 entries built from retrievable PA 211 items, the Forest Warren Human Services Family Resource Hub, the Inglis Warren County Quick Start guide, and the Warren County Resource Guide; each item checked against the organisation's own website. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Public web only — no organisation was contacted.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**
**Auditor: Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.**
**This audit was prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## Headline findings — corrections strong enough to send (own-site evidence)
1. **Lakeshore Community Services, Warren — use the intellectual-disability intake number, not the general Warren number.** PA 211 lists both its Warren residential and Family Living listings at (814) 723-4680. Lakeshore's own site (lakeshorecs.org, Mental Health Services / Intellectual Disabilities Services page, retrieved 2026-11-08) gives **"More Information or Intake: (814) 314-1184"** for its Intellectual Disabilities Services. PA 211 should add or substitute the 314-1184 intake number on the two Warren ID listings (1020 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA 16365).
2. **Don Mills Achievement Center — drop the old "250 Hospital Drive" address.** Don Mills' own site and the United Fund Warren agency list both give **677 Hospital Drive, Suite J, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-5730**. An older secondary source still circulates "250 Hospital Drive, North Warren, PA 16365" for Camp-Talk-A-Lot. That address should not be used; the current address is 677 Hospital Drive, Suite J.
3. **Two Centers for Independent Living both claim Warren County — say which one is local.** The PA CIL list and the Inglis Warren guide both list CRI and Voices for Independence as serving Warren. CRI has the Warren office: **1003 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 726-3404 / (866) 579-3404, info@crinet.org** (own site, crinet.org/locations/warren). VFI's office is in Erie: **1432 Wilkins Road, Erie, PA 16505, (814) 874-0064, TTY (814) 874-0064, toll-free (866) 407-0064** (own site, vficil.org). A Warren listing that gives only the VFI Erie address, or that implies VFI has a Warren office, should be corrected to name CRI as the Warren-based CIL and VFI as the Erie-based CIL that also serves Warren.
4. **Forest-Warren Human Services Developmental Disabilities — the direct number is (814) 726-8404, Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm, not just the main switchboard.** PA 211 itself lists this (285 Hospital Drive, Warren, PA 16365; voice (814) 726-2100). Keep both numbers, with 726-8404 labelled as the Developmental Disabilities direct line; do not present the main 726-2100 number as the only route.
5. **IU5 Early Intervention — publish the referral route, not just the main number.** IU5's own annual notices give the Edinboro office as **252 Waterford Street, Edinboro, PA 16412, (814) 734-5610**, and the Early Intervention referral route as **1-800-677-8461** (also shown as 1-800-677-5610 ext. 8461, eireferral@iu5.org). A listing that gives only 734-5610 without the EI referral number sends Warren parents to the general office first.
6. **Special Olympics Warren County local phone (814) 726-9760 is UNVERIFIED — do not publish it from this audit.** It appears only in an aggregator listing. No Warren-specific page, address, or contact was found on Special Olympics Pennsylvania's own site (specialolympicspa.org) in this check; the statewide HQ is Norristown, (610) 630-9450. PA 211 should confirm the Warren programme's current contact directly with SOPA before publishing a local number.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It is NOT a complete directory of Warren County disability services. PA 211's JavaScript search pages did not yield a complete Warren-filtered list by text fetch; this is a partial pilot (28 entries) anchored on retrievable items plus secondary lists.
- No organisation is called closed or defunct. A failed or missing page, a conflicting address, or an aggregator-only phone is reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure. Lifestyle Support Services (#13) is the clearest case: its own site is live and describes current services.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently operating, has capacity, or will accept a given person. Eligibility, waiting lists, and availability were not verified and are not claimed here except where the organisation's own site states them.
- Accessibility information itself can go stale: TTY numbers, relay routes, and email addresses in this report are as published on the check date (2026-11-08). The CRI Warren TTY gap (#1) and the VFI TTY-equals-voice number (#2) should be re-checked at send time, not assumed still current.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Several entries are regional (Erie, Pittsburgh, statewide) and are included only because an authoritative source names Warren in their service area; their addresses are outside Warren County and are labelled as such.
- No expert advisory content has been rewritten. This audit covers contact information only — names, addresses, phones, accessible contact routes, and who is served where stated. It gives no advice about disability, treatment, benefits eligibility, or which service a person should use.
- The auditor is not a local resident of Warren County.
