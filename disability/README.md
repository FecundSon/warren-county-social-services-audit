# Warren County, PA — Disability Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation
was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 in-scope disability services listings for
Warren County, PA, plus 1 out-of-county exclusion (28 pilot entries
total), checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and it should
not be used as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211
first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.

Warren County is rural, in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat
Warren, PA; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute,
Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick and
Clarendon. Several services in this audit are regional — based in
Erie, Pittsburgh, or statewide — and are included only because an
authoritative source names Warren County in their service area.
Their addresses are outside Warren County and are labelled as such.
ZIP code does not settle county; exact-address geocoding does.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state an address
  or phone, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is
  a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be
  published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page, a conflicting address, or an aggregator-only
  phone is reported as unverified, never as closure. Lifestyle
  Support Services is the clearest case: its own site is live and
  describes current services, while its Warren address and phone
  could not be confirmed from that site.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating, has capacity, or will accept a given person.
- Accessibility information itself can go stale: TTY numbers and
  email addresses are as published on the check date. No Warren-
  office TTY was found for CRI; VFI publishes its TTY as the same
  number as its voice line. Re-check both at send time.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete
  extractable Warren County disability result list. The pilot is
  therefore anchored on retrievable PA 211 items plus the Forest
  Warren Human Services Family Resource Hub "Disability & ODP"
  section, the Inglis Warren County Quick Start guide, and the
  Warren County Resource Guide, each item labelled with its claim
  source. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren
  County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections,
  challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations
  themselves are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
- This audit covers contact information only. It does not rewrite,
  summarise, or give advice about disability, treatment, benefits
  eligibility, or which service a person should use.
