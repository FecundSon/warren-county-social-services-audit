# Warren County, PA — Senior Services Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (rural, northwestern PA; county
seat Warren, PA).
**Category:** Senior services.
**Directory checked against:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org /
pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to
statewide PA 211, Experience Inc., or any organisation. No organisation
was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. Keith is NOT a
local resident of Warren County.

## Method and limits — please read before using this

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No organisation,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state
  an address, phone number or hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in
  this report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and
  must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page, and a missing listing, are reported as unverified,
  never as closure. A live website does not by itself prove a service is
  currently operating.
- Hours from a church/office/warehouse/centre page are not service hours,
  and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- Programme-level entries (meals, caregiver support, PA MEDI, protective
  services, care management) are services of one agency, Experience Inc.,
  the Area Agency on Aging for Warren and Forest Counties, reached
  through its main number. They are audited separately because directories
  list them separately.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete extractable
  Warren County-filtered result list. The pilot is therefore anchored on
  PA 211 items retrieved by targeted search, plus the statewide
  senior-centre list, the Warren & Forest Family Resource Hub, the VNA
  Alliance Warren/Forest resources list, and the organisations' own sites,
  each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send
  to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Forest County centres run by the same
  AAA are excluded as out of scope, not counted as Warren County
  services.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.

## Headline findings

1. **The two "Warren" senior-centre listings are one centre, not two —
   and one of them has the wrong address.** Directories list a "Warren
   Senior Community Center" at **905 4th Ave, Warren, PA 16365,
   (814) 723-4180** and a "Warren Senior Ctr Experience" at **42 Clark
   St, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-3237**. The Allegheny Community
   Center's own site puts it at **42 Clark Street, Warren, PA 16365**,
   phone **(814) 723-4180**, and 905 4th Ave is the Experience Inc.
   Area Agency on Aging office, not the centre. The 905 4th Ave entry
   should be corrected to 42 Clark St (or merged into the 42 Clark St
   entry), and the 723-3237 number is a second Experience Inc. number
   for the same centre, not a second centre.
2. **TAWC's phone number differs by one digit between sources.**
   Experience Inc's own centre pages give "TAWC of Warren County
   723-1875", but PA 211 and TAWC's own published information give
   **(814) 723-1874, 42 Clark Street, Warren**. PA 211 should confirm
   723-1874 with TAWC. Separately, Yellow Pages conflates TAWC with
   the Warren County Housing Authority and places it at **108 Oak St,
   Warren** — that is the Housing Authority's address, not TAWC's.
3. **"APPRISE" is the old programme name.** The Medicare counselling
   programme is now **PA MEDI** (Pennsylvania Medicare Education and
   Decision Insight), delivered in Warren County by Experience Inc. at
   905 4th Ave, (814) 723-3763, with a statewide helpline
   1-800-783-7067. Directory entries still calling it APPRISE are out
   of date.
4. **Tidioute Senior Center address: 174 Main Street, not 173.**
   Experience Inc's own centre page gives the Tidioute Senior Center
   as The Tidioute Towers, **174 Main Street, Tidioute, PA 16351**,
   (814) 484-3020, Mon/Tue/Thu 9am–3pm. An aggregator (seniorcenters.com)
   gives 173 Main St. The organisation's own site wins.
5. **Sugar Grove Senior Center: no current evidence — UNVERIFIED, not
   closed.** The only traces found are historical (a 2023 obituary
   recording a former cook/site supervisor there) and a bare "Senior
   Center" line in a Sugar Grove social-services listing. It does not
   appear on the statewide senior-centre list, which shows only
   Tidioute and the two Warren entries for Warren County. That is
   consistent with consolidation into the Warren/Sheffield/Tidioute
   centres, but it is not proof of closure, and this audit does not
   claim it closed.
6. **No Youngsville senior centre could be verified.** A single
   AI-generated local-services page claims APPRISE outreach sessions
   at senior centres in Youngsville and Tidioute, but no directory
   listing, address or phone for a Youngsville centre was found. Do not
   publish one. TAWC's Youngsville-area shared-ride service (Mon–Thu
   into Warren) is confirmed and is the verified Youngsville link.
7. **Senior Farmers Market vouchers: use the 2026 state list, not the
   centre's website.** The Allegheny Community Center site still shows
   a 2023 voucher notice (distribution July 10, 11 & 19, 2023).
   The Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture's **2026 Senior FMNP
   Agencies** list confirms Experience Inc. (905 4th Ave,
   (814) 723-3763) and the Allegheny Community Center ((814) 723-4180)
   as the Forest/Warren distribution points for 2026.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Warren County senior
  services. It is a partial pilot (25 in-scope listings) anchored on
  retrievable PA 211 items plus secondary lists and organisations' own
  sites. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren County is
  still outstanding.
- It does not claim the Sugar Grove Senior Center is closed, or that
  there is or is not a senior centre in Youngsville. Both are UNVERIFIED
  for lack of current public evidence, not findings of closure or
  absence.
- It does not claim any service is currently operating. A live website
  and a confirmed address/phone do not prove current operation.
- It does not report bed availability, waitlist position, fees beyond
  what the organisations themselves publish, or eligibility beyond what
  the organisations themselves state. TAWC fares are as published by
  TAWC/PA 211 and should be confirmed before travel.
- It does not rewrite or summarise any advisory content. Only contact,
  location, hours and programme-name facts were checked.
- It does not claim the auditor is a local resident of Warren County.
  He is not.
- Phone numbers were checked against published sources only. No number
  was dialled.
