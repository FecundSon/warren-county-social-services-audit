# Warren County, PA — Senior Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, Experience Inc., or any organisation.
No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 25 senior services listings for Warren County,
PA (a rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren, PA),
plus 4 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the organisations' own
websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a
replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
senior services, and it should not be used as one without checking with
the organisation or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of
Warren County.

Warren County's senior services are dominated by one organisation,
Experience Inc., the Area Agency on Aging for Warren and Forest Counties.
Most programme-level entries in this audit are services of that one
agency, reached through its main number, (814) 723-3763.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **The two "Warren" senior-centre listings are one centre, not two —
   and one has the wrong address.** Directories list a "Warren Senior
   Community Center" at **905 4th Ave, Warren, PA 16365,
   (814) 723-4180** and a "Warren Senior Ctr Experience" at **42 Clark
   St, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-3237**. The Allegheny Community
   Center's own site puts it at **42 Clark Street, Warren, PA 16365**,
   phone **(814) 723-4180**. 905 4th Ave is the Experience Inc. Area
   Agency on Aging office, not the centre. The 905 4th Ave entry should
   be corrected to 42 Clark St (or merged), and 723-3237 is a second
   Experience Inc. number for the same centre, not a second centre.
2. **TAWC's phone number differs by one digit between sources.**
   Experience Inc's own centre pages give "TAWC of Warren County
   723-1875", but PA 211 and TAWC's own published information give
   **(814) 723-1874, 42 Clark Street, Warren**. Yellow Pages also
   conflates TAWC with the Warren County Housing Authority and places
   it at 108 Oak St — that is the Housing Authority's address, not
   TAWC's.
3. **"APPRISE" is the old programme name.** The Medicare counselling
   programme is now **PA MEDI** (Pennsylvania Medicare Education and
   Decision Insight), delivered in Warren County by Experience Inc. at
   905 4th Ave, (814) 723-3763, statewide helpline 1-800-783-7067.
   Directory entries still calling it APPRISE are out of date.
4. **Tidioute Senior Center address: 174 Main Street, not 173.** The
   organisation's own centre page gives The Tidioute Towers,
   **174 Main Street, Tidioute, PA 16351, (814) 484-3020**,
   Mon/Tue/Thu 9am–3pm. An aggregator gives 173 Main St; the
   organisation's own site wins.
5. **Sugar Grove Senior Center: no current evidence — UNVERIFIED, not
   closed.** The only traces found are historical (a 2023 obituary
   recording a former cook/site supervisor there). It does not appear on
   the statewide senior-centre list. That is consistent with
   consolidation into the Warren/Sheffield/Tidioute centres, but it is
   not proof of closure, and this audit does not claim it closed.
6. **No Youngsville senior centre could be verified.** A single
   AI-generated local-services page claims APPRISE outreach sessions
   there, but no directory listing, address or phone was found. Do not
   publish one.
7. **Senior Farmers Market vouchers: use the 2026 state list, not the
   centre's website.** The Allegheny Community Center site still shows
   a 2023 voucher notice. The Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture's
   2026 Senior FMNP Agencies list confirms Experience Inc.
   (905 4th Ave, (814) 723-3763) and the Allegheny Community Center
   ((814) 723-4180) as the Forest/Warren distribution points for 2026.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state
  an address, phone number or hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in
  the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding,
  and must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page, and a missing listing, are reported as unverified,
  never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/centre page are not service hours, and are
  flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete
  extractable Warren County-filtered result list. The pilot is therefore
  anchored on PA 211 items retrieved by targeted search, plus secondary
  lists, each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check
  of search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any
  send to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county: exact-address geocoding does. Forest
  County centres run by the same AAA (Marienville, Endeavor) are
  excluded as out of scope.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
