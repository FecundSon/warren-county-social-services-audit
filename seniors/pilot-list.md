# Warren County, PA — Senior Services: Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (rural, northwestern PA; county seat
Warren, PA; other towns: Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar
Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon).
**Category:** Senior services (senior centres, meals, transport, benefits
help, caregiver support, in-home help).
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-08.
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. Keith is NOT a
local resident of Warren County.
**Status:** Pilot list only. Nothing in this audit has been sent anywhere.

## How this list was built

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch does not
return a complete Warren County-filtered result list, so this pilot is
anchored on PA 211 items retrievable by targeted search, plus the
statewide senior-centre list (carepennsylvania.org), the Warren & Forest
Family Resource Hub (Forest Warren Human Services), the VNA Alliance
Warren/Forest community resources list, and the organisations' own sites.
Each entry below records the directory claim as found, and its claim
source. It is a partial pilot, NOT a complete county directory.

Warren County's senior services are dominated by one organisation,
Experience Inc., the Area Agency on Aging (AAA) for Warren and Forest
Counties. Most programme-level entries below are services of that one
agency, reached through its main number. They are listed separately
because directories list them separately, and because a wrong number or
name on any one of them sends a caller to the wrong place.

ZIP code does NOT settle county: exact-address geocoding does. Forest
County centres run by the same AAA (Marienville, Endeavor) are listed
under Out of Scope, not counted as Warren County services.

## Method note

Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Public web only —
no organisation was contacted. Anything not confirmed from the
organisation's own site is marked UNVERIFIED in the audit report, never
published as fact. No organisation is called closed or defunct from a
failed page load or a missing listing.

This audit was prepared with AI assistance (Muse). The auditor is not a
local resident of Warren County.
