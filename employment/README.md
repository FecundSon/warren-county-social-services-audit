# Warren County, PA — Employment / Job Help Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No
organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 26 employment / job-help listings for Warren
County, PA (county seat Warren; other towns Youngsville, Sheffield,
Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick,
Clarendon), checked against the organisations' own websites and other
public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory:
it does not claim to be a current list of open job-help services, and
it should not be used as one without checking with the organisation
or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren
County. This audit was prepared with AI assistance (Muse).

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **PA CareerLink in Warren has no standalone walk-in office.**
   PA 211 lists "PA CareerLink - Career Development Warren County,
   24 Market Street, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-2350." That
   address and phone are real, but the region's own site
   (nwpajobconnect.org) lists no Warren full-service office: the
   only Warren public access point is **Warren Public Library,
   205 Market St, Mondays 10:00 am–3:00 pm**, toll-free
   1-844-333-5248. 24 Market St is space inside the Forest-Warren
   Human Services building, for adults and transition-age youth
   working with human-services providers, by appointment or drop-in
   there — not a general-public CareerLink office. Older
   aggregators still give a still older address, 1003 Pennsylvania
   Ave W, Suite 102, Warren — stale; do not use it.
2. **Warren Library CareerLink hours conflict.** NWPA Job Connect's
   own site says Mondays **10:00 am–3:00 pm**; PA CareerLink's own
   social graphics (May–September 2026) repeatedly say Mondays
   **10:00 am–2:30 pm** for the same Warren Library session. The
   own-site table is treated as authoritative here, but PA 211
   should confirm the closing time before publishing either.
3. **PathStone in Warren is not a local PathStone office.**
   PA 211 lists "PathStone Corporation - Senior Training and
   Employment Services, 200 Liberty Street, Warren, PA 16365,
   (585) 340-3300" (and the same programme again as "Senior
   Community Service Employment Program (SCSEP)"). 200 Liberty St
   is a host/training site address; PathStone's own site publishes
   no Warren street address or Warren local contact, and (585) is
   a Rochester, NY area code. The U.S. Department of Labor /
   CareerOneStop SCSEP finder lists only one Pennsylvania
   PathStone contact: 50 Main Street, Bradford, PA 16701,
   (814) 362-1855. The two PA 211 Warren listings are the same
   programme under two names — merge them, do not count them twice.
   The programme itself (low-income Pennsylvanians 55+, about
   20 hours/week paid community-service training) is confirmed by
   PA Department of Aging / PathStone descriptions.
4. **Goodwill Warren is at 4210 Market St, not the other Market St
   numbers in circulation.** PA 211 and Goodwill's own listing
   agree: **4210 Market Street (4210b), Warren, PA 16365,
   (814) 726-1328**, Sun 12–5pm, Mon–Sat 10am–7pm. Aggregators
   variously give 1079, 1666, 1905, and 4000 Market St — all
   stale/wrong. And store hours are not job-help hours: Goodwill
   Industries of North Central PA does provide supported
   employment / Employment Specialists in Warren, but no
   Warren-specific employment-services phone or hours are published;
   it is a referral route, not a walk-in job-help desk at the
   thrift store.
5. **Northern Pennsylvania Regional College (NPRC), Warren, is at
   300 2nd Avenue, Suite 500, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 230-9010**
   (also (844) 350-7729; workforcedev@nprc.edu) — confirmed on
   nprc.edu. Secondary sources give 6521 Route 6N and
   120 East 2nd Street, Warren — both wrong/stale for the admin
   office. NPRC has no traditional campus; classes meet in
   community venues.
6. **Bollinger / Barber National Institute, Bollinger Campus:
   (814) 723-8431 is the voice number; (814) 723-1882 is the fax.**
   Address **44 N State St, North Warren, PA 16365**. Several
   aggregators swap the two numbers. The campus serves adults with
   intellectual disabilities / autism (supported employment,
   pre-employment training); it is a Barber National Institute
   affiliate (affiliation completed July 1, 2020), so listings
   under the old "Bollinger Enterprises, Inc." / beiwarren.com
   name should point to barberinstitute.org.
7. **Warren County Assistance Office phone misprint in circulation.**
   Correct: **210 North Drive, Suite A, North Warren, PA 16365,
   (814) 723-6330** (toll-free 1-800-403-4043), Mon–Fri 8am–5pm.
   One secondary directory prints (814) 723-6630 — wrong digit.
8. **Warren County Career Center is grades 10–12 only.** 347 East
   5th Avenue, Warren, (814) 726-1260, wcsdpa.org. Its own site
   describes a secondary career-and-technical centre for grades
   10–12; no adult job-help programme was found on its own site.
   Do not list it as adult employment help.
9. **Warren County Chamber of Business & Industry is not a
   job-seeker service.** Current: **308 Market St, Warren,
   (814) 723-3050**, wccbi.org — a business-membership body whose
   workforce work is employer-side advocacy. Older listings give
   315 Second Ave and (814) 723-6024 — stale.
10. **Four listings on the pilot page are out of county and
    excluded:** PA CareerLink Erie County (Erie County), PA
    CareerLink Oil Region, Franklin (Venango County — and its old
    255 Elm St, Oil City address is also stale), Jamestown
    Community College, Jamestown, NY (Chautauqua County, NY), and
    Venango Training & Development Center, Seneca (Venango County;
    its PA 211 service area is Clearfield, Forest, Jefferson and
    Venango — Warren is not listed).

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not
  state a Warren address, phone, or hours, that item is marked
  UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.**
  A failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as
  closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating, and a programme's funding / eligibility can change
  without its web page changing.
- Hours from a library / store / school / office page are not
  job-help hours, and are flagged as such wherever that confusion
  was possible (Warren Public Library, Goodwill store, Warren
  County Career Center).
- Programme names are a duplicate risk in this category: the same
  service appears under a funder's name (AARP / NCOA for SCSEP),
  a programme name (PathStone SCSEP vs Senior Training and
  Employment Services; Hi-Ed School-to-Work vs the Hi-Ed Council
  itself), and a provider's name (CareerLink at the library, at
  24 Market St, and at the Forest-Warren Human Services building).
  Duplicates are flagged, not double-counted as two sources of help.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are
  JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return
  a complete extractable Warren County-filtered employment list
  (the statewide Employment and Job Search result pages were
  retrieved, but they mix all Pennsylvania counties on each page
  and only two Warren items could be isolated with confidence:
  PA CareerLink Warren County, 24 Market St, and PathStone,
  200 Liberty St). The pilot is therefore anchored on those two
  PA 211 items plus the county human-services plans, the NWPA Job
  Connect site, and secondary lists, each item labelled with its
  claim source. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for
  Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
  This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete county directory.
- ZIP code does not settle county: several Warren addresses share
  the 16365 ZIP across Warren city, North Warren, and Conewango
  Township. County was settled by exact street address, not ZIP.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections,
  challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the
  organisations themselves are welcome — that is the point of
  staging it here first.
