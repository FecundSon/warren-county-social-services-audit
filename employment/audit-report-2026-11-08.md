# Warren County, PA — Employment / Job Help Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; Youngsville,
Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren,
Starbrick, Clarendon).
**Category:** Employment / job help (job search, training, resume
help, benefits counselling).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing
checked against the organisation's own website; own site beats
directory; directory beats aggregator. No organisation was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this audit has been sent to
statewide PA 211 or any organisation.
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance (Muse). Keith is NOT a
local resident of Warren County.
**Source directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).

## Headline findings
Only the corrections strong enough to send (own-site evidence,
material to someone trying to use the service):

1. **PA CareerLink, Warren — no standalone walk-in office.**
   PA 211: "PA CareerLink - Career Development Warren County,
   24 Market Street, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-2350." The
   address/phone are real, but NWPA Job Connect's own site
   (nwpajobconnect.org/pa-careerlink-services/, checked 2026-11-08)
   lists no Warren full-service office. The only Warren public
   access point it lists is Warren Public Library, 205 Market St,
   Mondays 10:00 am–3:00 pm, toll-free 1-844-333-5248.
   24 Market St is CareerLink space inside the Forest-Warren Human
   Services building, for adults and transition-age youth working
   with human-services providers. Correct the listing to the
   access model, not just the address. The older aggregator address
   1003 Pennsylvania Ave W, Suite 102, Warren, is stale.
2. **Warren Library CareerLink hours conflict (10–3 vs 10–2:30).**
   NWPA Job Connect's own site table says Mondays 10:00 am–
   3:00 pm; PA CareerLink's own social graphics (May–September
   2026) repeatedly say Mondays 10:00 am–2:30 pm for the same
   session. Ask PA 211 / NWPA Job Connect to confirm the closing
   time before publishing either.
3. **PathStone, Warren — not a local office; two listings, one
   programme.** PA 211 lists the same programme twice at
   200 Liberty Street, Warren, PA 16365, (585) 340-3300 — once as
   "Senior Training and Employment Services" and once as "Senior
   Community Service Employment Program (SCSEP)". Merge them.
   PathStone's own site (pathstone.org) publishes no Warren street
   address or Warren local contact; (585) is a Rochester, NY area
   code. The U.S. Department of Labor / CareerOneStop SCSEP finder
   lists only one Pennsylvania PathStone contact: 50 Main Street,
   Bradford, PA 16701, (814) 362-1855. 200 Liberty St is a
   host/training site, not a PathStone office. The programme itself
   (low-income Pennsylvanians 55+, about 20 hours/week paid
   community-service training) is confirmed by the PA Department
   of Aging.
4. **Goodwill Warren — 4210 Market St, not the other Market St
   numbers.** PA 211 and Goodwill's own listing agree: 4210 Market
   Street (4210b), Warren, PA 16365, (814) 726-1328, Sun 12–5pm,
   Mon–Sat 10am–7pm. Aggregators give 1079, 1666, 1905, and
   4000 Market St — all stale/wrong. Store hours are not job-help
   hours: Goodwill NC PA's Warren employment help is via
   Employment Specialists / referral, with no Warren-specific
   employment-services phone or hours published.
5. **NPRC, Warren — 300 2nd Ave, Suite 500, not Route 6N or
   120 E 2nd St.** Confirmed on nprc.edu: 300 2nd Avenue,
   Suite 500, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 230-9010, (844) 350-7729,
   workforcedev@nprc.edu. Secondary sources give 6521 Route 6N
   and 120 East 2nd Street — both wrong/stale for the admin office.
6. **Bollinger Campus — (814) 723-8431 is voice; (814) 723-1882
   is fax.** 44 N State St, North Warren, PA 16365. Several
   aggregators swap them. List under Barber National Institute
   (Bollinger Campus), barberinstitute.org, not the legacy
   beiwarren.com / "Bollinger Enterprises, Inc." name alone.
7. **Warren County Assistance Office — (814) 723-6330, not
   (814) 723-6630.** 210 North Drive, Suite A, North Warren,
   PA 16365; toll-free 1-800-403-4043; Mon–Fri 8am–5pm. One
   secondary directory prints the 6630 misprint.
8. **Warren County Career Center is grades 10–12 only**
   (347 East 5th Avenue, Warren, (814) 726-1260). No adult
   job-help programme was found on its own site; do not present
   it as adult employment help.
9. **Warren County Chamber of Business & Industry is not a
   job-seeker service** (308 Market St, Warren, (814) 723-3050).
   Older listings (315 Second Ave; (814) 723-6024) are stale.
10. **Four out-of-county exclusions:** PA CareerLink Erie County
    (Erie County); PA CareerLink Oil Region, Franklin (Venango
    County; old 255 Elm St, Oil City address also stale);
    Jamestown Community College, Jamestown, NY (Chautauqua
    County, NY); Venango Training & Development Center, Seneca
    (Venango County; PA 211 service area Clearfield, Forest,
    Jefferson, Venango — Warren not listed).

### 1. PA CareerLink — Career Development Warren County
- **Directory claim (PA 211):** 24 Market Street, Warren, PA 16365;
  (814) 723-2350. "One-stop job center… job counseling, testing
  and assessment; resume preparation; interview training; job
  matching and referral; unemployment insurance and job
  registration; labor market and career information; financial
  aid for education and training; referral for job training."
- **Own-site claim (NWPA Job Connect, nwpajobconnect.org/
  pa-careerlink-services/):** No Warren full-service office
  listed. Services are free, funded by PA Dept. of Labor &
  Industry; in-person or virtual; locations in Clarion, Crawford,
  Erie, Forest, Venango, and Warren County. Only Warren entry in
  its Public Access Locations table: Warren Public Library,
  205 Market St, Warren, PA 16365, Mondays 10:00 am–3:00 pm.
  Toll-free 1-844-333-5248; info@nwpacareerlink.org.
- **Evidence:** PA 211 Job Search page (retrieved 2026-11-08)
  gives 24 Market St / (814) 723-2350; NWPA Job Connect own
  page lists only the library session for Warren. County Human
  Services Plan FY 2024–25 (pa.gov) says Forest-Warren Human
  Services "provides CareerLink with office space… open to adults
  and transition age youth working with human services providers"
  and that CareerLink can "set up an appointment, or can stop in"
  at the Human Services building. Old aggregator address
  1003 Pennsylvania Ave W, Suite 102 (globgov.com) is not on the
  NWPA site.
- **Verdict: CORRECTED** — address/phone are real, but the
  access model is wrong: this is not a standalone walk-in
  CareerLink office; it is appointment/drop-in space in the
  human-services building, with the library Monday session as
  the public access point.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. No
  organisation is called closed in this report; anything not
  confirmed from an own site is marked UNVERIFIED.
- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Warren County
  employment help. PA 211's Warren-filtered list could not be
  pulled completely by text fetch (see Method in README.md); this
  is a partial pilot anchored on two retrievable PA 211 Warren
  items plus county plans, the NWPA Job Connect site, and
  secondary lists.
- It does not claim current programme availability, funding,
  eligibility, or waitlist status. Training funding (e.g. WIOA
  training funds up to $5,500 in NWPA materials, Rev. 6.2024) and
  SCSEP income/age rules change; confirm with the provider.
- It does not present library hours, store hours, school hours, or
  office hours as job-help hours.
- It does not reproduce or rewrite employment, benefits, or
  training advice — contact information and access routes only.
- ZIP code was not used to settle county; exact street addresses
  were used. 16365 covers Warren city, North Warren, and
  Conewango Township.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating.
