## How this list was built
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and a text fetch on
2026-11-08 did not return a complete Warren County-filtered
employment list: the statewide Employment and Job Search pages mix
all Pennsylvania counties, and only two Warren items could be
isolated with confidence from them (marked PA 211 below). The rest
of the pilot is anchored on the county human-services plans, the
NWPA Job Connect site, and secondary lists, each item labelled with
its claim source. This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete county
directory. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren
County is still outstanding before any send. ZIP code does not
settle county — exact street address does.
