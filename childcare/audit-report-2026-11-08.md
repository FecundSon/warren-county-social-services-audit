# Warren County, PA — Child Care / Family Support Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide PA 211 or to any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.

## What this is

A small, dated audit of 24 child care / family support listings for Warren County, PA, plus 3 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open providers, and it should not be used as one without checking with the provider or PA 211 first.

## Headline findings (only the corrections strong enough to send)

1. **Early Learning Resource Center region is wrong in aggregators.** Warren County is served by **ELRC Region 2** (Northwest Institute of Research), **5 Beaver Drive, DuBois, PA 15801, (814) 503-0988, toll-free 855-445-0202** — not Region 1 (Erie) and not Region 5, as aggregator pages claim. Region 2 serves Cameron, Clarion, Clearfield, Elk, Forest, Jefferson, McKean, Potter and Warren counties (pa.gov ELRC page; NWIR programmes page). Sending Warren families to Region 1 or Region 5 is a wrong-door error.
2. **North Warren Presbyterian Community Day Care — phone digit transposition.** CountyOffice lists **814-723-3644**; the church's own listing and other aggregators give **814-723-6441** (3 Church St, North Warren, PA 16365; church office 814-723-5060, nwpchurch.org). The 3644 number transposes the middle digits. One aggregator also labels the day care "Permanently Closed" — that label is **not** supported by the church's own site, which still describes the Day Care Ministry and Community House tenancy, so this audit does **not** call it closed; current operation is UNVERIFIED on the day-care's own current terms and should be confirmed with the church.
3. **First Presbyterian Church, Warren — Creative Preschool address.** The enrolment form header says **200 Market St**, but the church's own site and bulletins consistently give **300 Market St, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-9200** (warrenfpc.org). Use 300 Market St. The form's 200 Market St is stale/wrong. Preschool itself is confirmed as running: Tue/Wed/Thu, Sept–May, 3–4-year-olds 8:20–11:15am, Pre-K 8:30–11:30am, tuition $1,260 for 2026–27, Pre-K Scholarship via Warren-Forest EOC / PA EITC.
4. **Warren County YMCA — facility hours are not child-care hours.** The YMCA's own homepage (warrenymca.org, checked 2026-11-08) gives **facility** hours for Warren (Mon–Fri 5:00am–9:00pm, Sat 7:00am–7:30pm, Sun 12:00–4:00pm) and Youngsville (Mon–Fri 6:00am–8:00pm, Sat 8:00am–1:00pm, closed Sunday). No child-care ages or child-care hours were stated on the own-site pages retrieved. The national YMCA directory page lists the Warren Y as "Mon–Sun: Closed", which is plainly stale/wrong against the Y's own current hours. Do not publish facility hours as child-care hours.
5. **H.R. Rouse Children's Center — own site contradicts itself on closing time.** The centre's own page (rouse.org/care/rouse-childrens-center/) says **Mon–Fri 6:00am–5:30pm**; its own FAQ page (rouse.org/care/rouse-childrens-center/faqs/) says **Mon–Fri 6:00am–6:00pm**. Address (238 E Main St, Youngsville, PA 16371), phone (814-563-2890) and ages (12 months–12 years, Keystone STAR 4) agree across the own site. The closing time needs one authoritative correction from Rouse; directories quoting either time are quoting Rouse, not inventing it.
6. **Key Moments Childcare Center — hours conflict, no own website.** The centre's Facebook page (its only web presence found) gives **Mon–Fri 6:30am–5:30pm**; daycare.com gives **Mon–Fri 8:00am–5:30pm**. Address 677 Hospital Dr, Warren, PA 16365 and phone (814) 723-5730 agree. A 3rd-anniversary post on 2026-10-01 shows the centre active in October 2026. Hours need confirming with the centre; do not publish either set as settled.
7. **Family Services of Warren County — two addresses circulate; they are two different sites.** PA 211 lists **589 Hospital Drive, Suite E, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-1330** (counselling). Other sources list **119 Market St, Warren, PA 16365** — that is the Recovery Center site (announced hours Aug 2026: Mon 10am–8pm, Tue 8am–4pm, Wed 10am–6pm, Thu 8am–4pm, Fri 8am–2pm). The own site (fswc.org, checked 2026-11-08) confirms phone (814) 723-1330, ages 5 and up, and office hours Mon & Thu 8am–6pm, Tue & Wed 8am–8pm, Fri 8am–2pm, but does not state a street address on the page retrieved. Do not collapse the two addresses into one.
8. **Head Start — one phone, one age cut-off, six Warren County centres.** All six Warren County centres enrol through Warren-Forest EOC at **(814) 726-2400** (1209 Pennsylvania Ave W, Warren). A Head Start Facebook flyer (2026-08-12) gives a different enrolment number, **814-230-0974**, and states children must have turned **3 by July 1**. Centre addresses are as listed in the table below. Current session days/hours per centre were not stated on the own/agency pages retrieved — do not publish a centre schedule from the 2011–12 annual report (four days a week, five hours a day), which is out of date.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **No provider is called closed or defunct.** Creative Learning Adventures, Care With Grace and North Warren Presbyterian Community Day Care each carry a single aggregator "closed" label or a stale licence record; none of that is own-site evidence of closure, and each is marked UNVERIFIED (or, for North Warren, CORRECTED on phone with operation unverified), never as a closure.
- **No waitlist position, bed/place availability, or eligibility outcome is stated.** No waitlist status was stated on any own site retrieved; where a subsidy route is named (Child Care Works via ELRC Region 2, Head Start, Pre-K Counts, WIC), only the route and the administering body are reported, not whether a given family qualifies.
- **No schedule is published from a church/office/facility page as a child-care schedule.** YMCA facility hours, church office hours and the FSWC Recovery Center hours are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- **This is not a complete Warren County child-care directory.** PA 211's JavaScript search pages did not return a complete extractable Warren County-filtered list in text fetch on 2026-11-08. Home-based family child care providers are under-represented here (only the aggregator-listed names were visible; home addresses are not published in this audit). School-year vs summer hours were not verified for most centres; several programmes run school-year schedules (e.g. Creative Preschool Sept–May; Head Start session calendar) and summer provision differs.
- **No children's personal information or photos are included.** None was collected.
- A live website or Facebook page does not by itself prove a provider is currently operating.

## Method and limits

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- A provider's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party directories. Where an own site did not state an address or hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED in the table — a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and it must not be published as fact.
- PA 211 items retrieved directly in text: Warren County Children's Advocacy Center; Domestic Relations of Warren County; Forest-Warren Human Services — Children's Protective Services. All other pilot items are anchored on secondary lists (county government pages, Forest-Warren Family Resource Hub, Head Start directories) and are labelled with their claim source in `pilot-list.md`.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges and updates from statewide PA 211 or the providers themselves are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
