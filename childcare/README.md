# Warren County, PA — Child Care / Family Support Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or to any provider. No organisation
was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 child care / family support listings for
Warren County, PA (rural, northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren,
PA), plus 3 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the providers' own
websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a
replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
providers, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
provider or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren
County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)

   **ELRC Region 2** (Northwest Institute of Research), **5 Beaver
   Drive, DuBois, PA 15801, (814) 503-0988, toll-free 855-445-0202** —
   not Region 1 (Erie) and not Region 5, as aggregator pages claim.
   Region 2 serves Cameron, Clarion, Clearfield, Elk, Forest,
   Jefferson, McKean, Potter and Warren counties.
2. **North Warren Presbyterian Community Day Care — phone digit
   transposition.** CountyOffice lists 814-723-3644; the correct number
   is **814-723-6441** (3 Church St, North Warren, PA 16365; church
   office 814-723-5060). A single aggregator "Permanently Closed"
   label is not supported by the church's own site, so this audit does
   not call it closed.
3. **First Presbyterian Church, Warren — Creative Preschool address.**
   The enrolment form header says 200 Market St, but the church's own

   Sept–May, ages 3–4 and Pre-K, tuition $1,260 for 2026–27.

   Own site (warrenymca.org): Warren facility Mon–Fri 5:00am–9:00pm,

   6:00am–8:00pm, Sat 8:00am–1:00pm, closed Sunday. No child-care ages

   YMCA directory page listing Warren as "Mon–Sun: Closed" is stale.
5. **H.R. Rouse Children's Center — own site contradicts itself on
   closing time.** Its centre page says Mon–Fri 6:00am–**5:30pm**; its

   Youngsville, PA 16371), phone (814-563-2890) and ages (12 months–

6. **Key Moments Childcare Center — hours conflict, no own website.**
   Facebook (its only web presence found) gives Mon–Fri

7. **Family Services of Warren County — two addresses, two sites.**

8. **Head Start — one phone, one age cut-off, six Warren County

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A provider's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state an address
  or hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED in the full
  report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must
  not be published as fact.
- **No provider is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page, a stale licence record, and single aggregator
  "closed" labels are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website or Facebook page does not by itself prove a provider
  is currently operating.
- Hours from a church/office/facility page are not child-care hours,
  and are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- School-year vs summer hours were not verified for most centres;
  several programmes run school-year schedules and summer provision
  differs. No waitlist status was stated on any own site retrieved.
- No children's personal information or photos are included.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete
  extractable Warren County-filtered child-care result list (two PA 211
  items were retrieved directly: Warren County Children's Advocacy
  Center and Domestic Relations of Warren County, plus Forest-Warren
  Human Services / Children's Protective Services). The pilot is
  therefore anchored on those PA 211 items plus secondary lists, each
  item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Warren County child care is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the providers themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
