# Warren County, PA — Social Services Listing Audits

**All categories checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged for publication in
Keith's own repo first. Nothing in this audit has been sent to PA 211
or any organisation as a direct message.**

## What this is
One county, twelve public categories — a dated curation audit of
public social-services listings for Warren County, PA (a rural county
in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren, PA), checked against
the organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is not
a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open
services, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
organisation or PA 211 first.

Each category folder holds three files: a README with the headline
corrections, the full audit report with evidence and sources, and the
original pilot list it was checked against.

The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County. This audit was
prepared with assistance from an AI assistant, Muse. Every report
documents its provenance and limits; nothing was invented, imputed, or
deleted silently, and no organisation is called closed or defunct from
a failed page load — anything not confirmed from the organisation's
own site is marked UNVERIFIED.

## Headline corrections (one per category)
- **Food:** Divergent Church distributes Mon/Wed/Fri (food arrives
  8:30–11:30am), not Fridays only; Sheffield "Area" and "Ecumenical"
  pantries are one pantry at 18 Leather St; Youngsville "2nd Street"
  and "Ministerium" pantries are one pantry at 18 2nd St.
- **Shelters:** PA 211's Erie VAMC Homeless Care Program listing at
  3 Farm Colony Drive, Warren, is the VA mental-health outpatient
  clinic, not a shelter; veterans should be sent to the VA National
  Call Center for Homeless Veterans, 1-877-424-3838. Faith Inn's
  income limit is now below 80% AMI, not 0–30%.
- **Mental health / crisis:** The Forest Warren crisis line is
  **1-844-757-3224, text 63288, 24/7, plus mobile crisis** (CCR) —
  PA 211 shows CCR's Clarion admin office instead.
- **Housing:** Habitat for Humanity's Warren phone is (814) 688-8671,
  not the (229) Georgia number aggregators use; the Housing Authority
  waitlist status is unresolved between two secondary sources and is
  not published either way.
- **Utilities:** LIHEAP's 2026–27 season is open (Nov 2, 2026 –
  Apr 23, 2027); the Warren County Assistance Office number is
  (814) 723-6330, not (814) 723-6630; Dollar Energy Fund's Penelec
  maximum grant is $600, not $500.
- **Employment:** PA CareerLink has no standalone walk-in office in
  Warren — the public access point is Warren Public Library on
  Mondays; PathStone Warren is one programme listed twice.
- **Child care:** Warren County is ELRC **Region 2** (NWIR, DuBois),
  not Region 1 (Erie) or Region 5; North Warren Presbyterian day
  care phone is 814-723-6441 (digit transposition).
- **Seniors:** "Warren Senior Community Center" (905 4th Ave) and
  "Warren Senior Ctr Experience" (42 Clark St) are both the Allegheny
  Community Center at **42 Clark St, (814) 723-4180**; "APPRISE" is
  now **PA MEDI**.
- **Disability:** Lakeshore Community Services ID intake is
  (814) 314-1184, not (814) 723-4680; Don Mills Achievement Center
  is at 677 Hospital Drive, Suite J, not 250 Hospital Drive.
- **Substance use:** Deerfield Centers for Addiction Treatment
  (DCAT) Warren is at **1885 Market Street, 814-723-5545**, not
  514 West Third Avenue.
- **Legal aid:** Northwestern Legal Services Franklin office is
  Suite **406**, not 420; the PBA Lawyer Referral Service number is
  **800-932-0311**, not 800-692-7375.
- **Transportation:** TAWC's phone is (814) 723-1874 (Experience Inc.
  lists 723-1875); no current TAWC fixed-route fare is published —
  circulating figures ($0.75 / $1.00 / $1.25) conflict.

## What this audit does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Items
  that could not be confirmed from an organisation's own site are
  marked UNVERIFIED, not closed.
- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Warren County
  services. See "Honest limit" above.
- Bed availability, waitlist positions, and seasonal status are
  date-stamped to 2026-11-08 and change constantly; check with the
  organisation before relying on them.
- Fares and schedules are date-stamped to 2026-11-08.
- It does not rewrite expert advisory content (medical, mental-health,
  substance-use, or legal advice). Only contact information,
  structure, and metadata were audited.
