# Warren County, PA — Transportation Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA; other towns:
Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren,
Starbrick, Clarendon). Rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania.
**Category:** Transportation. **Date built:** 2026-11-08.
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. Keith is NOT a local
resident of Warren County.

## What this list is

The candidate list this audit was checked against: 27 entries (19 in scope for
Warren County, PA, plus 8 out-of-scope exclusions). Each entry records the
directory's own claim first; the audit report
(`audit-report-2026-11-08.md`) records what the organisation's own website
actually says and the verdict.

**Limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a
text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete extractable
Warren-County-filtered transportation result list. One PA 211 record was
retrieved in full (Transit Authority of Warren County), plus PA 211 taxonomy
search pages for Neighborhood Shuttle Services, Non-Emergency Medical
Transportation, Transport for People with Disabilities, Senior Ride Programs,
and Errand Running/Shopping Assistance. This audit is therefore a **partial
pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items + secondary lists + organisations'
own sites, NOT a complete county directory.** A live-browser check of
search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send.

## Pilot list — out of scope (8)

### 20. Forest County Transportation / CATA (Forest County)
- **Why excluded:** "This service is for Forest County Residents Only"
  (co.forest.pa.us). Office: 128 Cherry Street, Marienville, PA 16239,
  (814) 927-8266, toll-free 1-800-222-1706. Operated by CATA (Crawford Area
  Transportation Authority) under contract effective July 1, 2026. Its MATP

  service. Warren County residents use TAWC.

### 21. CATA — fixed-route bus service, Meadville and Titusville (Crawford County)

### 27. Prompt Care Transportation LLC (Grove City, PA)
- **Why excluded:** Non-emergency medical transport based in Grove City, PA
  (phone 724-977-0049). Its advertised service area (Shenango Valley, Youngstown,

## Not included, and why
- **Sears Luxury Transport** (Warren, PA): private luxury hire (airport transfers,
  weddings, corporate travel), not a social-service listing.
- **Uber / Lyft in Warren, PA:** rideshare availability in Warren County was not
  verified from any retrievable source in this check; do not list as available.
- **Warren County School District buses:** school transport, not a public
  social service.
- **Warren Manor (682 Pleasant Drive, Warren):** a health-care facility whose own
  service list includes "Transportation" for its residents — facility-internal,
  not a public ride service.
- **Medical Answering Services (MAS):** New York State Medicaid transport broker.
  Pennsylvania residents use MATP, not MAS — see item 12.
