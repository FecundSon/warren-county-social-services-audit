# Warren County, PA — Transportation Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, TAWC, or any provider. No
organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 19 transportation listings for Warren County, PA
(county seat Warren; Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove,
Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon), plus 8 out-of-county
exclusions, checked against the providers' own websites and other public
listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not
claim to be a current list of transport options, and it should not be used
as one without checking with the provider or PA 211 first. The auditor is
NOT a local resident of Warren County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Wrong phone digit for TAWC on Experience Inc.'s senior pages.**
   Experience Inc. lists the TAWC partnership number as **723-1875**; TAWC's
   own site and PA 211 both give **(814) 723-1874**.
2. **No current fixed-route fare is published anywhere retrievable.**
   TAWC's own site (routes effective July 1, 2026) publishes routes and stops
   but no fare; PA 211 says only "Fees may apply." Circulating figures
   conflict: **$0.75** (undated brochure), **$1.00** (travel aggregator),
   **$1.25** (first-hand rider report, March 3, 2026). PA 211 and TAWC should
   publish the current fare.
3. **The $0.75 figure is a shared-ride senior rate, not the bus fare.**
   Experience Inc.'s pages give 65+ riders "$0.75 per trip in Warren County
   and a maximum of $3.90 outside of Warren County" via its TAWC partnership.
   Seniors 65+ ride Pennsylvania fixed-route buses free with a senior transit
   ID; $0.75 is the shared-ride rate.
4. **PA 211's service area for TAWC reads "Warren, PA" — it should read
   Warren County, PA.** TAWC is the public transportation provider for all of
   Warren County: three fixed routes (North-South, Sheffield, Youngsville)
   radiating from the City of Warren along US Routes 6 and 62, plus shared-ride
   countywide.
5. **MATP is a Medical Assistance programme, not a bus service.**
   Warren County MATP is administered through the TAWC station (42 Clark
   Street, Warren, (814) 723-1874) for Medical Assistance consumers going to
   medical appointments, including out-of-county. It must be listed separately
   from fixed-route and shared-ride service.
6. **Deerfield DCAT address conflict.** Deerfield Behavioral Health's own site
   lists its Warren office at **1885 Market St, Warren, PA 16365**, while
   several directories list **1003 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA 16365**
   (also the address PA 211 gives for Community Resources for Independence).
   The free-transport offer itself is confirmed on DCAT's own site: "FREE
   Transportation is available in Warren County" for DCAT clients.
7. **Heritage medical transport serves Warren County, PA — but its own service
   page says Chautauqua County.** Heritage's September 2026 post and advertising
   say its wheelchair-accessible non-emergency medical transport serves
   Chautauqua County (NY), Erie County (NY & PA), and Warren County (PA),
   (716) 338-0133 — but its medical-transport page opens "for Chautauqua
   County residents!" Rates are quoted per trip (cash or check at pickup;
   $30 cancellation fee; rates effective 1/1/2026). Pennsylvania residents
   should use MATP, not New York's Medical Answering Services (MAS), for
   Medicaid rides.
8. **Warren Taxi Service, LLC is NOT reported as closed**: a business-listing
   aggregator labels "Warren Taxi" (111 Walnut St, (814) 723-2020) as
   "Permanently Closed," but the only primary record found is a 2014 PUC
   filing. Operating status is UNVERIFIED, not a closure.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A provider's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where a provider's own site did not state a fare, booking lead
  time, or terms, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a
  gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No provider is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page, and a single aggregator "permanently closed" label (Warren
  Taxi Service, LLC), are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently operating.
- Fares and schedules are date-stamped 2026-11-08. Routes and schedules change
  with new contracts: TAWC's current schedules took effect July 1, 2026, and
  CATA's Forest County contract also took effect July 1, 2026.
- Medical transport is not general public transport, and vice versa. MATP
  (Medical Assistance only), Heritage (non-emergency medical, per-trip quotes),
  DCAT (clients only), the DAV vans (veterans to VA appointments), and Caring
  For Life (reimbursement, not a ride) each serve a defined group.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Several look-alikes in this pilot are for
  Warren County in Ohio (Lebanon/Dayton numbers) or Missouri (Warrenton) —
  same county name, different state. Exact-address geocoding settles county.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete extractable
  Warren-County-filtered transportation result list (one PA 211 record was
  retrieved in full: Transit Authority of Warren County, plus PA 211 taxonomy
  pages). The pilot is therefore anchored on that PA 211 record plus secondary
  lists, each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send
  to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges, and
  updates from statewide PA 211 or the providers themselves are welcome —
  that is the point of staging it here first.
