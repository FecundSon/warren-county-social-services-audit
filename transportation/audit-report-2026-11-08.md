# Warren County, PA — Transportation Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA).
**Category:** Transportation.
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08. All fares, schedules, and phone numbers below are
as found on 2026-11-08.
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Method:** Each pilot-listing was checked against the organisation's own
website. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator. Public web only
— no organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, TAWC, or any provider. No organisation was contacted.**
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. Keith is NOT a local
resident of Warren County, PA.

## Headline findings

Only the corrections strong enough to send (own-site evidence, material to
someone trying to use the service):

1. **Wrong phone digit for TAWC on Experience Inc.'s senior pages.** Experience
   Inc.'s senior-center pages list the TAWC partnership number as **723-1875**;
   TAWC's own site (tawcbus.com) and PA 211 both give **(814) 723-1874**.
   One digit differs — a caller using the Experience Inc. number will not reach
   TAWC. (Experience Inc., experienceinc.org/endeavor, checked 2026-11-08;
   TAWC, tawcbus.com, checked 2026-11-08.)
2. **No current fixed-route fare is published anywhere retrievable.**
   TAWC's own site (schedules page, routes effective July 1, 2026) publishes
   routes and stops but **no fare**. PA 211 says only "Fees may apply."
   The figures circulating conflict: an undated TAWC brochure (hosted on a
   third-party site) says **$0.75**; an AI-generated travel aggregator says
   **$1.00**; a first-hand rider report from March 3, 2026 says the Sheffield
   fare was **$1.25**. PA 211 and TAWC should publish the current fare.
   (Fare claims date-stamped 2026-11-08; none is confirmed from TAWC's own site.)
3. **The $0.75 figure is a shared-ride senior rate, not the bus fare.**
   Experience Inc.'s pages say 65+ riders travel "at a reduced rate of .75 per
   trip in Warren County and a maximum of $3.90 outside of Warren County" via
   its TAWC partnership. That is the shared-ride senior rate. Seniors 65+ ride
   Pennsylvania fixed-route buses free with a senior transit ID. Listing $0.75
   as the TAWC bus fare (as the old brochure and some aggregators do) misleads
   riders in both directions.
4. **PA 211's service area for TAWC reads "Warren, PA" — it should read
   Warren County, PA.** TAWC is the public transportation provider for all of
   Warren County (PennDOT performance review, April 2026): three fixed routes
   radiating from the City of Warren along US Routes 6 and 62, plus shared-ride
   countywide. The city-only service area understates the coverage.
5. **MATP is a Medical Assistance programme, not a bus service — list it
   separately.** Warren County MATP (Medical Assistance Transportation Program)
   is administered through the TAWC station (42 Clark Street, Warren,
   (814) 723-1874) for Medical Assistance consumers going to medical
   appointments, including out-of-county. It is not general public transport,
   and it must not be confused with TAWC fixed-route or shared-ride service.
6. **Deerfield DCAT address conflict.** Deerfield Behavioral Health's own site
   lists its Warren office at **1885 Market St, Warren, PA 16365**
   (deerfieldbehavioralhealth.org/addictions), while several directories list
   **1003 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA 16365** for the same programme.
   (1003 Pennsylvania Avenue West is also the address PA 211 gives for
   Community Resources for Independence.) PA 211 should confirm which Warren
   address is current for DCAT. The free-transport offer itself is confirmed on
   DCAT's own site: "FREE Transportation is available in Warren County" for
   DCAT clients (wheelchair-accessible, door-to-door, Forest and Warren Counties).
7. **Heritage medical transport serves Warren County, PA — but its own service
   page says Chautauqua County.** Heritage Ministries' September 9, 2026 post
   and its advertising say its wheelchair-accessible non-emergency medical
   transport serves Chautauqua County (NY), Erie County (NY & PA), and Warren
   County (PA), phone (716) 338-0133. Its own medical-transport page, however,
   opens: "Heritage Ministries provides non-emergency medical transportation
   for Chautauqua County residents!" Rates are quoted per trip (cash or check at
   pickup; $30 cancellation fee; rates effective 1/1/2026). A Warren County
   resident must call for a quote — and should use Pennsylvania MATP, not New
   York's Medical Answering Services (MAS), for Medicaid rides.
8. **Do NOT publish "Warren Taxi Service, LLC — permanently closed."** A
   business-listing aggregator labels "Warren Taxi" (111 Walnut St, Warren, PA
   16365, (814) 723-2020) as "Permanently Closed." The only primary record found
   is a 2014 Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission filing. A single aggregator
   label is not evidence of closure. This audit marks it UNVERIFIED, not closed.

## What this report does NOT claim

- **It is not a complete directory of Warren County transport.** PA 211's search
  pages are JavaScript-driven and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a
  complete Warren-County-filtered list. This is a partial pilot: 19 in-scope
  listings anchored on one full PA 211 record, PA 211 taxonomy pages, secondary
  lists, and organisations' own sites. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org
  for Warren County is still outstanding before any send.
- **No organisation is called closed or defunct.** A failed or missing page, and
  a single aggregator "permanently closed" label (Warren Taxi Service, LLC),
  are reported as UNVERIFIED, never as closure.
- **A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently operating.**
  TAWC's site, for example, confirms routes effective July 1, 2026, but publishes
  no fare — the fare figures in circulation ($0.75 / $1.00 / $1.25) are not
  confirmed from TAWC's own site.
- **Fares and schedules are date-stamped 2026-11-08, not current facts.**
  Routes and schedules change with new contracts — TAWC's current schedules
  took effect July 1, 2026, and CATA's Forest County contract also took effect
  July 1, 2026. Re-check before relying on or sending any fare or schedule.
- **Medical transport is not general public transport, and vice versa.**
  MATP (Medical Assistance only), Heritage (non-emergency medical, per-trip
  quotes), DCAT (clients only), the DAV vans (veterans to VA appointments), and
  Caring For Life (reimbursement, not a ride) each serve a defined group.
  Listing any of them as a general bus service would mislead riders.
- **ZIP code does not settle county.** Several look-alike listings in this
  pilot are for Warren County in Ohio (Lebanon/Dayton numbers, (937)/(513))
  or Missouri (Warrenton) — same county name, different state. Exact-address
  geocoding settles county; a "Warren County" name alone does not.
- **No treatment, medical, or eligibility advice is given.** Contact details
  only. The DAV, MATP, and DCAT entries describe who each service is for; that
  is not a determination that any individual qualifies.
- **No organisation was contacted.** Everything here comes from public web
  pages retrieved 2026-11-08.

## Cross-county confusion found in this check (worth flagging to PA 211)

- needhelppayingbills.com's "Warren and Forest County assistance programs" page
  mixes Warren County, Ohio's Community Services agency (phone (937) 425-2100)
  into a Warren County, PA page, and repeats the Ohio agency's senior
  transportation description as if it applied in Pennsylvania.
- bestplaces.net "Warren County, PA Commuting" pages claim the Port Authority of
  Allegheny County "provides bus service throughout Warren County" and that
  "Conneaut Valley Transit Commission runs two public bus routes covering
  northwest Pennsylvania," and describe a "Warren County Transit (WCT)" with
  "five routes." None of that matches TAWC (three fixed routes; the county's
  public provider since 1980). AI-generated aggregator pages are not evidence.
- Tripping.com's Cherry Grove page says the TAWC fixed-route fare "is only $1."
  That figure is not confirmed from TAWC's own site (see finding 2).
