# Warren County, PA — Legal Aid Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation
was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 legal aid listings relevant to Warren County,
PA (county seat Warren; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield,
Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, and
Clarendon), plus 5 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a curation
audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current
list of available legal help, and it should not be used as one without
checking with the organisation or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a
local resident of Warren County.

Warren County is rural and thinly served: the only free civil legal aid
office physically in the county is Northwestern Legal Services' Warren
office. Nearly everything else is a statewide helpline or a regional
office in Erie, Pittsburgh, Harrisburg, Philadelphia, or York. That
thinness is itself a finding — a Warren County resident looking for
free legal help will mostly be calling somewhere else.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Northwestern Legal Services, Franklin office — suite number.**
   PA 211 lists "1243 Liberty Street, Suite 420, Franklin, PA 16323."
   NWLS's own site lists **Suite 406**. (The Franklin office serves
   Venango County, not Warren County directly; Warren residents use
   the Warren office or central intake.)
2. **Pennsylvania Bar Association Lawyer Referral Service — phone
   number.** The PBA's own site gives **800-932-0311 (select option
   2)**, with an online form available 24/7 and a 30-minute
   consultation costing no more than $30. The old number
   **800-692-7375** still appears in Warren County court notices and
   on the Pennsylvania Health Law Project's own referrals page. Warren
   County is confirmed in the PBA LRS service area (PALawHELP entry,
   last reviewed Oct 8, 2026).
3. **Pennsylvania Institutional Law Project, Western office — address
   and phone.** PILP's own site gives **247 Fort Pitt Blvd, 4th Floor,
   Pittsburgh, PA 15222, 412-434-6004**. Aggregators list 100 Fifth
   Avenue, Pittsburgh, (412) 434-6175. PILP's eastern office has also
   moved: own site gives 718 Arch Street, Suite 304 South,
   Philadelphia, PA 19106, 215-925-2966 — older listings still show
   247 S Juniper St. Intake is primarily by mail.
4. **NWLS intake hours conflict — the most important clarification.**
   PA 211 says "Offices open 9am–5pm, Intake open 9am–4pm."
   PALawHELP (last reviewed Apr 21, 2020) says new clients should call
   **Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–2:00 PM**. NWLS's own site states **no
   intake hours at all** on its homepage or locations page. A Warren
   County resident cannot tell from the public web when to call. The
   new-client number is the Central Intake Unit, **800-665-6957**
   (or 814-452-6957), not the Warren office number (814) 726-2530,
   and **walk-ins are not accommodated** (NWLS family law page).
5. **PA 211 shows the full 10-county NWLS service area on every NWLS
   office listing.** NWLS's own site assigns each office specific
   counties: the Warren office serves Cameron, Elk, Forest, and
   Warren; Erie serves Erie only; Franklin serves Venango only;
   Meadville serves Crawford only; Bradford serves McKean and Potter;
   Hermitage serves Mercer only. Warren residents should use the
   Warren office or central intake, not the other offices directly.
6. **A Safe Place (Warren County) — use the hotline, not the office
   number, in a crisis.** PA 211 lists the office, (814) 726-1271,
   210 North Drive, Suite C, Warren. The organisation's own site
   gives the 24/7 hotline as **814-726-1030 or toll-free
   1-800-338-3460**. Only the public office address is given here;
   the shelter location is not published in this audit.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not
  state an address, phone, or hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED
  in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a
  finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating or taking new cases.
- Office hours are not intake hours, and are flagged as such wherever
  that confusion was possible. NWLS, Disability Rights Pennsylvania,
  and the PA Health Law Project all have intake hours that are
  narrower than, or simply not stated alongside, their office hours.
- **Domestic violence handling:** A Safe Place's shelter location is
  not published here and was not sought. Only its public office
  address (which the organisation itself publishes) and its hotline
  appear. No safe-house address appears anywhere in this audit.
- **ZIP code does not settle county.** Several listings found for
  "Warren County" searches belong to Warren County, New Jersey, or to
  neighbouring Pennsylvania counties (Erie, McKean, Elk, Butler).
  Those are excluded as out of scope in the full report, not treated
  as Warren County services.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete
  extractable Warren County-filtered result list. Retrievable PA 211
  items (NWLS offices, Domestic Relations, A Safe Place, and the
  Butler office of Neighborhood Legal Services) anchor the pilot,
  alongside secondary lists (PALawHELP, Justia, court-reference
  pages) and organisations' own sites, each item labelled with its
  claim source. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren
  County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves
  are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
