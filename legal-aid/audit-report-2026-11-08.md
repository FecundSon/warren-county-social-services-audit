# Warren County, PA — Legal Aid Listing Audit Report

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren, PA; towns include
Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake,
North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon)
**Category:** Legal aid
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing
checked against the organisation's own website. Own site beats
directory; directory beats aggregator. No organisation was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this report has been sent to
statewide PA 211 or any organisation. The auditor (Keith Conklin) is
NOT a local resident of Warren County.
**AI assistance:** This audit was prepared with assistance from Muse,
an AI assistant. All findings are drawn from the public sources cited
per item.

## Headline findings
Only the corrections strong enough to send (own-site evidence,
material to someone trying to use the service):

1. **NWLS Franklin office suite is wrong in PA 211.** PA 211:
   "1243 Liberty Street, Suite 420, Franklin, PA 16323." NWLS own
   site (nwls.org/our-locations): **Suite 406**.
2. **PBA Lawyer Referral Service number has changed.** PBA own site
   (pabar.org): **800-932-0311, select option 2**; online form 24/7;
   30-minute consultation no more than $30; covers Warren County
   (PALawHELP entry, last reviewed Oct 8, 2026). The old number
   **800-692-7375** still appears in Warren County court notices
   (e.g. a 2022 quiet-title notice) and on PHLP's own referrals
   page. Whether the old number still connects was not tested.
3. **PILP Western office address and phone are wrong in
   aggregators.** PILP own site (pilp.org): **247 Fort Pitt Blvd,
   4th Floor, Pittsburgh, PA 15222, 412-434-6004**; intake primarily
   by mail. Aggregators list 100 Fifth Avenue, Pittsburgh,
   (412) 434-6175. Eastern office moved from 247 S Juniper St to
   **718 Arch Street, Suite 304 South, Philadelphia, PA 19106,
   215-925-2966**.
4. **NWLS intake hours cannot be established from the public web.**
   PA 211 says intake open 9am–4pm; PALawHELP (last reviewed
   Apr 21, 2020) says new clients call Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–2:00 PM;
   NWLS's own site states no intake hours. New clients call the
   Central Intake Unit, **800-665-6957** (or 814-452-6957), not the
   Warren office line, and **walk-ins are not accommodated**.
5. **PA 211 overstates each NWLS office's service area.** Every NWLS
   office listing in PA 211 carries all 10 counties. NWLS's own site
   assigns each office specific counties; the Warren office covers
   Cameron, Elk, Forest, and Warren. Warren residents should use the
   Warren office or central intake.
6. **A Safe Place: the 24/7 hotline is not the number PA 211 leads
   with.** PA 211 lists the office, (814) 726-1271. Own site gives
   the 24/7 hotline **814-726-1030 / 1-800-338-3460**. Office address
   (210 North Drive, Suite C, Warren) confirmed; shelter location
   not published here.

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim any organisation is closed, defunct, or no
  longer serving Warren County. Nothing was found closed; anything
  not confirmed is marked UNVERIFIED, which is a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding.
- It does not claim to be a complete directory of legal help for
  Warren County. PA 211's Warren County-filtered list could not be
  pulled completely by text fetch (see Method). This is a partial
  pilot: 24 in-scope listings anchored on retrievable PA 211 items,
  secondary lists, and organisations' own sites.
- It does not reproduce or rewrite legal advice. Only contact
  information, service scope, eligibility, and intake routes were
  audited.
- It does not publish any confidential location. A Safe Place's
  shelter location is not in this report; only its public office
  address and hotline appear, as the organisation itself publishes
  them.
- It does not present office hours as intake hours. Where the two
  differ, or where intake hours are unstated, that is flagged
  rather than resolved.
- It does not claim ZIP code proves county. Several excluded
  listings share Warren County place names or ZIP prefixes with
  other counties or with Warren County, New Jersey.
- Phone numbers were not called. A number listed as "old" or
  "stale" (e.g. PBA 800-692-7375) is stale against the
  organisation's own current site; whether it still connects was
  not tested.
