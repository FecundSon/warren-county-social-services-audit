# Warren County, PA — Utilities Assistance Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA).
**Category:** Utilities assistance (LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds).
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, any utility, or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse, from
public web sources only.

## Method and limits — please read before using this

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No organisation,
  utility, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state a phone number,
  address, or window, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in this report — it is
  a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be published
  as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a programme is currently
  operating or funded.
- **Seasonal windows are date-stamped.** LIHEAP's 2026–27 season runs
  November 2, 2026 through April 23, 2027 (PA DHS, confirmed 2026-11-08).
  Any listing still describing LIHEAP as closed, or showing a 2025–26
  window, is stale. No Warren County listing in this pilot was found
  stating LIHEAP is closed.
- **Partial-pilot limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are
  JavaScript-driven. A text fetch of search.pa211.org for "utilities"
  with a Warren County filter on 2026-11-08 returned a statewide,
  unfiltered result set (Philadelphia, Lancaster, Chambersburg, Sunbury,
  and other counties) with no Warren County entry visible in the fetched
  text. A targeted PA 211 search for "LIHEAP Warren County" the same day
  returned exactly one Warren County entry (item 1). The pilot is therefore
  anchored on that retrievable PA 211 item plus the utilities and
  community-action programmes that serve Warren County, each labelled with
  its claim source in the pilot list. This is NOT a complete county
  directory. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren County is
  still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- Two fetch attempts during this check hit the page-fetch service's rate
  limit (429) and were not retried: the FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Assistance
  Programs PDF and National Fuel's payment-assistance parent page on the
  first attempt. The FirstEnergy PDF's contents are cited from the
  provider's own document as returned by search; the National Fuel parent
  page was successfully fetched on a later attempt. Items affected are
  flagged in their evidence lines.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Every in-scope item was checked against
  its street address / service territory, not its ZIP code. Warren County,
  Ohio is a recurring trap in secondary sources (see item 22 and the notes
  below) and was excluded, not corrected into the PA list.

## Headline findings

1. **PA 211's Warren County LIHEAP listing is correct for the 2026–27
   season.** PA 211 lists the PA DHS Northwestern County Assistance Office
   LIHEAP 2026–2027 Season at 210 North Drive, Suite A, N. Warren, PA
   16365, (814) 726-2540, grants $200–$1,000 with crisis grants. PA DHS's
   own LIHEAP page confirms the season is open November 2, 2026 through
   April 23, 2027, grants $200–$1,000, hotline 1-866-857-7095. No
   correction to PA 211 is needed on this entry.
2. **Warren County Assistance Office main number: 814-723-6330, not
   814-723-6630.** The NWIR Early Learning Resource Center county-offices
   page lists 814-723-6630. National Fuel's own LIHEAP-locations page, the
   COMPASS listing, Community Care Behavioral Health's Forest & Warren
   resource guide, and multiple directories all give 814-723-6330 (toll
   free 1-800-403-4043). The 6630 figure looks like a transposed digit in
   one secondary directory. PA 211 itself lists the LIHEAP line
   814-726-2540 for this office, which is consistent across sources, so
   this is a correction for the NWIR page, not for PA 211.
3. **Dollar Energy Fund maximum grant for Penelec customers is $600, not
   $500.** The fund's own FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Hardship Program page
   states a $600 maximum (one grant per utility per programme year,
   Oct 1–Sep 30). Aggregator paenergyratings.com states $500. The fund's
   own page also gives the current seasonal windows: Oct 1–Nov 30 (service
   off or in threat of termination), Dec 1–Jan 31 (service off only),
   Feb 1–Feb 28 (off or in threat), from Mar 1 all eligible applicants.
   On the check date (Nov 8, 2026) the programme is in its Oct 1–Nov 30
   window.
4. **National Fuel's own payment-assistance page still shows a COVID-era
   Emergency Rental Assistance Program as "open through September 2025."**
   September 2025 has passed; the page as fetched on 2026-11-08 still
   presents the programme as open through that date. That is a stale
   listing on the utility's own site, for National Fuel to correct — it is
   not a PA 211 listing error. The same page lists the Pennsylvania
   Homeowner Assistance Fund (PAHAF) with a February 1, 2022 launch date
   and "until funds are exhausted" and no current status; its current
   status is UNVERIFIED in this audit, not claimed closed.
5. **National Fuel's CAP is no longer called LIRA on its own site.**
   National Fuel's income-based gas discount programme is now the
   Customer Assistance Program (CAP); "LIRA" (Low-Income Residential
   Assistance Program) survives in PUC filings and secondary guides such
   as the Utility Law Project Desk Guide 2025. Directories still listing
   "LIRA" should be read as the same programme, not a second one. Apply
   via 1-800-365-3234 (Catch-up Coaches, 7 a.m.–6 p.m. Mon–Fri, confirmed
   on National Fuel's own page) or nationalfuel.com/applycap.
6. **National Fuel's Neighbor for Neighbor Heat Fund page lists WFEOC as
   the Warren/Forest affiliate** (1209 Pennsylvania Ave West, PO Box 547,
   Warren, PA 16365, 814-726-2400) — confirmed. The same page, however,
   shows the Erie number 814-459-4581 next to the Butler Salvation Army
   entry, which appears to be a copy error on National Fuel's page (the
   Butler office's own number is not 814-459-4581). That error is outside
   Warren County, but it means the page should be re-checked in full
   before anyone relies on its affiliate numbers.
7. **Salvation Army Warren utility help is confirmed only at division
   level, not on the corps' own page.** The Warren corps' own page
   confirms phone 814-723-8950 and hours Mon–Fri 9 AM–2 PM, but its
   services section did not load in the fetch and its street address
   (311 Beech St) comes from secondary sources only. The Western
   Pennsylvania Division page confirms utility assistance across Western
   PA and Dollar Energy Fund partner agencies, but does not name Warren
   specifically. Do not publish a Warren-specific utility-assistance
   claim for the corps from this audit alone.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does not claim to be a complete directory of Warren County utilities
  assistance. It is a partial pilot (21 in-scope items) anchored on one
  retrievable PA 211 item plus the serving utilities and the county's
  community action agency. A live-browser PA 211 check is still
  outstanding.
- It does not claim any organisation or programme is closed or defunct.
  Items 9, 17, and 19 are UNVERIFIED — gaps in public web evidence, not
  findings — and must be confirmed directly with the organisations, not
  published from this audit.
- It does not claim fund availability. Dollar Energy Fund, Neighbor for
  Neighbor, and LIHEAP crisis grants are first-come, first-served and
  donation- or appropriation-limited; a programme listed as open can run
  out of funds mid-season.
- It does not claim the CAO's opening time: sources conflict between
  8:00 AM and 8:30 AM. Do not publish either as fact from this audit.
- It does not claim Salvation Army Warren offers utility assistance on the
  strength of this audit alone (item 16): confirmed only at division
  level, with the corps' own services section unloaded.
- It does not rewrite or summarise any advisory content. Only contact
  information, programme names, windows, eligibility as stated, and
  application routes were audited.
- It does not treat a ZIP code as proof of county. Warren County, Ohio
  listings were excluded on address/area-code evidence.
- Keith is not a local resident of Warren County, and no organisation was
  contacted for this audit.
