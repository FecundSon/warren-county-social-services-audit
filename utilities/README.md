# Warren County, PA — Utilities Assistance Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, any utility, or any organisation. No
organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 21 utilities assistance listings for Warren County,
PA (county seat Warren, PA; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield,
Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick,
Clarendon), plus 1 out-of-scope exclusion, checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public sources. It is a curation
audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list
of open programmes, and it should not be used as one without checking with
the organisation or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of
Warren County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **PA 211's Warren County LIHEAP listing is correct for the 2026–27
   season** — PA DHS Northwestern County Assistance Office LIHEAP,
   210 North Drive, Suite A, N. Warren, PA 16365, (814) 726-2540,
   grants $200–$1,000 with crisis grants. PA DHS's own LIHEAP page
   confirms the season is open November 2, 2026 through April 23, 2027,
   hotline 1-866-857-7095. No correction to PA 211 is needed on this entry.
2. **Warren County Assistance Office main number is 814-723-6330, not
   814-723-6630.** The NWIR Early Learning Resource Center county-offices
   page lists 814-723-6630; National Fuel's own LIHEAP-locations page,
   the COMPASS listing, CCBH's Forest & Warren guide, and multiple
   directories all give 814-723-6330 (toll free 1-800-403-4043). The 6630
   figure looks like a transposed digit in one secondary directory. PA 211
   itself lists the LIHEAP line 814-726-2540, which is consistent, so this
   is a correction for the NWIR page, not for PA 211.
3. **Dollar Energy Fund maximum grant for Penelec customers is $600, not
   $500.** The fund's own FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Hardship Program page
   states a $600 maximum (one grant per utility per programme year,
   Oct 1–Sep 30). Aggregator paenergyratings.com states $500. Current
   windows on the fund's own page: Oct 1–Nov 30 (service off or in threat
   of termination), Dec 1–Jan 31 (off only), Feb 1–Feb 28 (off or threat),
   from Mar 1 all eligible applicants.
4. **National Fuel's own payment-assistance page still shows a COVID-era
   Emergency Rental Assistance Program as "open through September 2025."**
   September 2025 has passed; the page as fetched on 2026-11-08 still
   presents it as open through that date. Stale listing on the utility's
   own site, for National Fuel to correct — not a PA 211 error.
5. **National Fuel's income-based gas discount programme is now the
   Customer Assistance Program (CAP), not LIRA.** "LIRA" survives in PUC
   filings and secondary guides; directories still listing "LIRA" are
   describing the same programme, not a second one. Apply via
   1-800-365-3234 (Catch-up Coaches, 7 a.m.–6 p.m. Mon–Fri) or
   nationalfuel.com/applycap.
6. **National Fuel's Neighbor for Neighbor Heat Fund page lists WFEOC as
   the Warren/Forest affiliate** (1209 Pennsylvania Ave West, PO Box 547,
   Warren, PA 16365, 814-726-2400) — confirmed. The same page shows the
   Erie number 814-459-4581 next to the Butler Salvation Army entry, an
   apparent copy error on National Fuel's page.
7. **Salvation Army Warren utility help is confirmed only at division
   level, not on the corps' own page.** The Warren corps' own page
   confirms phone 814-723-8950 and hours Mon–Fri 9 AM–2 PM, but its
   services section did not load in the fetch and its street address
   (311 Beech St) comes from secondary sources only. Do not publish a
   Warren-specific utility-assistance claim for the corps from this audit
   alone.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No organisation,
  utility, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state a phone number,
  address, or window, that item is marked UNVERIFIED in the full report —
  it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be
  published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a programme is currently
  operating or funded. Dollar Energy Fund, Neighbor for Neighbor, and
  LIHEAP crisis grants are first-come, first-served and can run out of
  funds mid-season.
- Seasonal windows are date-stamped. LIHEAP's 2026–27 season runs
  November 2, 2026 through April 23, 2027 (PA DHS, confirmed 2026-11-08).
  No Warren County listing in this pilot was found stating LIHEAP is closed.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch of search.pa211.org for "utilities" with a Warren County
  filter on 2026-11-08 returned a statewide, unfiltered result set with no
  Warren County entry visible in the fetched text. A targeted PA 211 search
  for "LIHEAP Warren County" returned exactly one Warren County entry
  (item 1). The pilot is therefore anchored on that retrievable PA 211 item
  plus the utilities and community-action programmes that serve Warren
  County, each labelled with its claim source. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send.
- Two fetch attempts during this check hit the page-fetch service's rate
  limit (429) and were not retried: the FirstEnergy Pennsylvania Assistance
  Programs PDF and National Fuel's payment-assistance parent page on the
  first attempt. The FirstEnergy PDF's contents are cited from the
  provider's own document as returned by search; the National Fuel page was
  successfully fetched on a later attempt. Items affected are flagged in
  the full report.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Warren County, Ohio listings (notably a
  CornerHelp entry with a (513) Cincinnati area code, and Ohio HEAP
  Winter/Summer Crisis programmes mixed into a secondary Warren/Forest PA
  page) were excluded on address and area-code evidence, not corrected
  into the PA list.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211, the utilities, or the organisations
  themselves are welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
