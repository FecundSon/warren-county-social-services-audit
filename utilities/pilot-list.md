# Warren County, PA — Utilities Assistance Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA; other towns
include Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake,
North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon).
**Category:** Utilities assistance (LIHEAP, shut-off help, fuel funds).
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-08.
**Status:** Pilot list only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to PA 211,
any utility, or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.
**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse, from
public web sources only.

## How this list was built

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven. A text fetch of
search.pa211.org for "utilities" with a Warren County filter on 2026-11-08
returned a statewide, unfiltered result set (Philadelphia, Lancaster,
Chambersburg, Sunbury, and other counties) with no Warren County entry
visible in the fetched text. A targeted PA 211 search for "LIHEAP Warren
County" on the same day returned exactly one Warren County entry: the PA
DHS Northwestern County Assistance Office LIHEAP 2026–2027 Season listing
(item 1 below). Because a complete Warren County-filtered PA 211 list could
not be pulled by text fetch, this pilot is anchored on that retrievable
PA 211 item plus the utilities and community-action programmes that serve
Warren County, each labelled with its claim source below. It is a partial
pilot, NOT a complete county directory. A live-browser check of
search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send.

Warren County's energy utilities, confirmed from the utilities' own sites:
electricity is Penelec (FirstEnergy); natural gas is National Fuel Gas.
The county's community action agency is the Warren-Forest Counties
Economic Opportunity Council (WFEOC), 1209 Pennsylvania Avenue West,
Warren, PA 16365, (814) 726-2400.

## Open questions before checking (answered in the audit report)

1. Is the Warren County LIHEAP entry on PA 211 current for the 2026–27
   season? — Yes: PA 211 labels it "LIHEAP - 2026 - 2027 Season" and the
   grant range matches PA DHS.
2. Which Warren CAO phone number is right — 814-723-6330 or 814-723-6630?
   — Consensus (National Fuel own site, COMPASS listing, CCBH, multiple
   directories) is 814-723-6330; the NWIR Early Learning Resource Center
   page is the outlier with 814-723-6630.
3. Does National Fuel still call its CAP "LIRA"? — Its current name is
   Customer Assistance Program (CAP); LIRA survives in PUC filings and
   secondary guides.
4. Is there a Warren County Dollar Energy Fund intake agency? — Not
   established from own sites in this check; Salvation Army Warren is a
   Western PA Division location and the division says many locations are
   Dollar Energy Fund partner agencies, but Warren is not named as one.
   Left UNVERIFIED, not assumed.
5. Is the Salvation Army Warren utility assistance confirmed on its own
   page? — Only at division level; the corps' own page services section
   did not load in the fetch. See audit report item 16.
