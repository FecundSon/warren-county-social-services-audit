# Warren County, PA — Housing / rental assistance audit

- County: Warren County, PA (rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren, PA; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon)
- Category: Housing / rental assistance
- Date checked: 2026-11-08
- Method: public web only — browser search and page-text fetch. No live browser tasks, no sign-ins, no purchases, no contact with any organisation. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
- Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org)
- Status: **NOT SENT** — staged only. Nothing in this report has been submitted to PA 211 or to any organisation.
- Auditor note: the auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County. No residency claim is made anywhere in this report.

## Headline findings

1. **Warren County's rent help is thin and concentrated in two places.** The county Homeless Assistance Program (HAP) — one month's rent, security deposits, mortgage arrears — is delivered by just two organisations, the Warren-Forest Counties Economic Opportunity Council (EOC) and the Salvation Army Warren. Everything else in the pilot is subsidised housing with waitlists, supportive housing for restricted populations, counselling, or legal help — not general rent money.
2. **The Housing Authority waitlist status is genuinely unresolved and must not be published either way.** AffordableHousingHub (verified October 14, 2026) says the Housing Choice Voucher and public housing waitlists are open; CornerHelp (confirmed October 20, 2026) lists the same voucher programme as "Funds out for now." The Housing Authority's own site (hacwpa.com, checked 2026-11-08) states no waitlist status on the pages retrieved. Any directory entry claiming the Warren waitlist is open — or closed — on the basis of one secondary tracker is overstating its evidence.
3. **Habitat for Humanity's phone is wrong in the aggregators.** Publichousing.com and shelterlistings.org list (229) 924-6935 for Habitat for Humanity of Warren County, PA. 229 is a Georgia area code, used identically for Habitat affiliates across many states. The Inglis / PHFA Warren County guide, Warren Gives and GreatNonprofits all give (814) 688-8671, PO Box 1473, Warren, PA 16365, info@habitatwarrenpa.org. The (229) number must be corrected, not repeated.
4. **The County Assistance Office has a phone and a street-name conflict.** (814) 723-6630 (pa.gov CAO directory / NWIRLRC) vs. (814) 723-6330 (benefits aggregators); "210 North Drive" vs. "210 North Street", North Warren, PA 16365. The toll-free 1-800-403-4043 agrees everywhere. Neither local number nor street name should be published as settled without a re-check. The Emergency Shelter Allowance dollar figures ($400/$300) come from a 2023 state slide deck, not a same-day DHS retrieval.
5. **The Salvation Army in Warren is one centre, not three listings.** Only 311 Beech Street, (814) 723-8950, Monday–Friday 9 AM–2 PM, is the Worship & Service Center on the Salvation Army's own site. Aggregator listings at 218 Liberty Street ((814) 726-3455) and 4210 Market Street ((814) 406-0000) are thrift-store locations. A directory that lists all three as rent-help intakes is triple-counting one provider.
6. **Presidential Place is mislabelled as senior housing.** lowincomeapartments.us describes it as low-income seniors housing. Its own mission (via Charity Navigator / HANDS) is 8 affordable homes in a group-home setting for adults experiencing chronic mental illness, at 105 Madison Avenue, Warren — the same HANDS manager and phone ((814) 453-3333) as Warren Anthems. It is population-restricted supportive housing, not general senior housing and not a rent fund.
7. **HAP is one programme, not two funds — and its old dollar caps are stale.** The 2014–15 county Human Services Plan set caps of up to $400 (Salvation Army) and up to $300 (EOC) per rental-assistance request. Those figures are more than a decade old and are NOT published here as current. The EOC and the Salvation Army are the two delivery points of the same county programme.
8. **COVID-era rental assistance is over and must not be listed as current help.** The EOC was the Warren County ERAP contact in DHS lists dated 2021–2022 (COMPASS applications). ERAP is ended; its successor in Warren County is HAP (items 5 and 12).
9. **"Warren County" is a trap name in this pilot.** Family Promise of Warren County is in Warren County, New Jersey; "Seed Sowers of Warren County" (765 area code) is in Warren County, Indiana; Bradmar Village, Crestline Villa and Ernest G. Anderson Manor are in Jamestown, NY; a (937) 425-2100 "Warren County Community Services" number is Ohio; and Warren Metropolitan Housing Authority flyers are Ohio. All are excluded as OUT OF SCOPE. A Warren postal address or a "Warren County" name alone does not settle county — exact addresses were used.
10. **Most of the pilot is counselling, legal help, or restricted housing — not rent money.** Northwestern Legal Services (eviction-prevention legal help), the EOC's HUD-approved counselling and HEMAP/foreclosure work, Habitat (homeownership building), and the supportive properties (Warren Anthems, Presidential Place, PATH, permanent supportive housing) all help with housing stability, but none of them pays a general rent bill. Directories that file them all under "rent payment assistance" mislead people in crisis about where money can come from.

## What this report does NOT claim

- This is NOT a complete directory of Warren County housing/rental assistance. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and the text fetch returned a statewide Housing Authorities list, not a Warren County-filtered list (one Warren record — HACW HCV — was retrieved from it). The pilot was anchored on that PA 211 item plus the Inglis / PHFA Warren County Quick Start guide and organisations' own sites. A partial pilot is never presented here as complete. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- No "funds available", waitlist-open, or application-open status is claimed as current unless the organisation's own site said so on 2026-11-08. In particular: the HACW voucher/public-housing waitlist, HAP funding availability, ESA amounts, and the Allegheny Pointe / Buchanan Court waitlists are NOT claimed.
- No organisation is claimed to be closed or defunct. Failed or incomplete page loads were recorded as UNVERIFIED, never as closure — including Catholic Charities Counseling Warren, where one place source carries a "Temporarily Closed" label.
- Dollar caps taken from old or secondary sources only (HAP $300/$400 from the 2014–15 county plan; ESA $400/$300 from 2023 state slides; Family Promise-style caps do not apply here) are flagged as such and must be re-checked before any send.
- Addresses and phones with logged conflicts (HACW Canterbury Court phone; County Assistance Office phone and street name; Habitat phone) are NOT settled in this report.
- No residency claim is made about the auditor. Choosing Warren County for audit does not establish residency there.
- Nothing here has been sent to PA 211 or to any organisation, and no organisation was contacted during this check.
