# Warren County, PA — Housing / rental assistance — pilot list

- County: Warren County, PA (rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren, PA; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon)
- Category: Housing / rental assistance
- Date checked: 2026-11-08
- Directory: statewide PA 211 (pa211.org / search.pa211.org), plus clearly-labelled secondary lists where PA 211 text fetch was incomplete
- Status: NOT SENT — staged only, nothing submitted to PA 211 or any organisation
- Note: This is a pilot, not a complete directory. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven; a text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned the statewide Housing Authorities taxonomy page (which did include the Warren County Housing Authority HCV record) but no complete Warren County-filtered list. Warren items below were extracted from that page and cross-checked against organisations' own sites where possible, anchored also on the Inglis / PHFA Warren County Quick Start Housing guide. Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
- Auditor note: the auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County. Choosing Warren County for audit does not establish residency there.

## How to read this list

- "Directory claim" = what PA 211 or a secondary list said.
- "Own-site check" = what the organisation's own site (or an official city/state page) said on 2026-11-08.
- Verdicts: CONFIRMED / CORRECTED / CLARIFIED / UNVERIFIED / OUT OF SCOPE.
- No funding, waitlist or "funds available" claim is presented as current unless the organisation's own site said so on the check date.
- No organisation is called closed/defunct on the basis of a failed page load.
- ZIP code does NOT settle county — exact-address geocoding does. Several "Warren County" traps in this pilot are other states' Warren Counties or New York properties (see OUT OF SCOPE).
