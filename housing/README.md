# Warren County, PA — Housing / Rental Assistance Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No
organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 24 housing / rental assistance listings for
Warren County, PA — a rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania
(county seat Warren, PA; other towns include Youngsville, Sheffield,
Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick
and Clarendon) — plus 6 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a
curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be
a current list of open rent-help sources, and it should not be used
as one without checking with the organisation or PA 211 first. The
auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Housing Authority waitlist status is unresolved — do not publish
   it either way.** AffordableHousingHub (verified October 14, 2026)
   says the Housing Choice Voucher and public housing waitlists are
   open; CornerHelp (confirmed October 20, 2026) lists the same
   voucher programme as "Funds out for now." The Housing Authority's
   own site (hacwpa.com, checked 2026-11-08) states no waitlist
   status on the pages retrieved. Address and phone are confirmed:
   108 Oak Street, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-2312.
2. **Habitat for Humanity's phone is wrong in the aggregators.**
   Publichousing.com and shelterlistings.org list (229) 924-6935 —
   a Georgia area code, used identically for Habitat affiliates in
   many states. The correct number, from the Inglis / PHFA Warren
   County guide, Warren Gives and GreatNonprofits, is
   **(814) 688-8671**, PO Box 1473, Warren, PA 16365,
   info@habitatwarrenpa.org. Habitat builds and sells homes; it
   does not pay rent.
3. **County Assistance Office phone and street conflict.** (814)
   723-6630 (pa.gov CAO directory) vs. (814) 723-6330 (benefits
   aggregators); "210 North Drive" vs. "210 North Street", North
   Warren, PA 16365. Toll-free 1-800-403-4043 agrees everywhere.
   Neither local number nor street name should be published as
   settled without a re-check. Emergency Shelter Allowance dollar
   figures ($400/$300) come from 2023 state slides, not a same-day
   DHS retrieval.
4. **The Salvation Army in Warren is one centre, not three
   listings.** Only **311 Beech Street, (814) 723-8950,
   Monday–Friday 9 AM–2 PM** is the Worship & Service Center on
   the Salvation Army's own site. Aggregator listings at 218
   Liberty Street ((814) 726-3455) and 4210 Market Street
   ((814) 406-0000) are thrift-store locations, not rent-help
   intakes.
5. **Presidential Place is mislabelled as senior housing.**
   Lowincomeapartments.us calls it low-income seniors housing. Its
   own mission is 8 affordable homes in a group-home setting for
   adults experiencing chronic mental illness, 105 Madison Avenue,
   Warren, managed by HANDS ((814) 453-3333) — the same manager
   and phone as Warren Anthems, 116 Muir Street.
6. **Canterbury Court phone conflict.** (814) 723-2312 (Housing
   Authority main number) vs. (814) 723-2131 (publichousing.com)
   for the 62-and-over HUD-subsidised building at 310 Liberty
   Street, Warren. The 62+ restriction is confirmed on the
   Housing Authority's own site; the phone is not settled.
7. **HAP is one programme, not two funds — and its old caps are
   stale.** The county Homeless Assistance Program (one month's
   rent, deposits, mortgage arrears) is delivered by the
   Warren-Forest Counties Economic Opportunity Council and the
   Salvation Army together. Caps of up to $400 (Salvation Army)
   and up to $300 (EOC) in the 2014–15 county plan are more than
   a decade old and must not be sent as current.
8. **COVID-era rental assistance is over.** The EOC was the Warren
   County ERAP contact in DHS lists dated 2021–2022. ERAP is
   ended; its successor in Warren County is HAP.
9. **"Warren County" is a trap name.** Family Promise of Warren
   County is in Warren County, New Jersey; "Seed Sowers of Warren
   County" (765 area code) is in Warren County, Indiana; Bradmar
   Village, Crestline Villa and Ernest G. Anderson Manor are in
   Jamestown, NY; a (937) 425-2100 "Warren County Community
   Services" number is Ohio. All are excluded as OUT OF SCOPE.
10. **Most of the pilot is not rent money.** Northwestern Legal
    Services (eviction-prevention legal help), the EOC's
    HUD-approved counselling and HEMAP/foreclosure work, Habitat
    (homeownership), and the supportive properties (Warren Anthems,
    Presidential Place, PATH, permanent supportive housing) help
    with housing stability, but none pays a general rent bill.
    Filing them all under "rent payment assistance" misleads
    people in crisis about where money can come from.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No
  organisation, PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not
  state an address, phone or status, that item is marked UNVERIFIED
  in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a
  finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A
  failed or missing page, and a single place-source
  "Temporarily Closed" label (Catholic Charities Counseling,
  Warren), are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a programme is currently
  operating or funded.
- Hours from a church/office/centre page are not service hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible. Funding
  and waitlist statuses change frequently; none is presented as
  current unless the organisation's own site said so on the check
  date.
- ZIP code does not settle county — exact addresses were used, and
  six "Warren County" listings from other states (and Jamestown,
  NY) were excluded as OUT OF SCOPE.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are
  JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned a
  statewide Housing Authorities list, not a complete extractable
  Warren County-filtered result list (one Warren record was
  retrieved from it: the Housing Authority HCV record). The pilot
  is therefore anchored on that PA 211 item plus the Inglis / PHFA
  Warren County Quick Start Housing guide and organisations' own
  sites, each item labelled with its claim source. A live-browser
  check of search.pa211.org for Warren County is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections,
  challenges, and updates from statewide PA 211 or the
  organisations themselves are welcome — that is the point of
  staging it here first.
