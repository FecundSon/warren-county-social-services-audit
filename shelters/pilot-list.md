# Warren County, PA — Shelters Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA; other towns:
Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren,
Starbrick, Clarendon). Rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania. Neighbouring
counties: Erie, Crawford, Forest, McKean, Venango (PA) and Chautauqua /
Cattaraugus (NY). **ZIP code does NOT settle county — exact-address
geocoding does.**

**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-08. **Status: not sent yet. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## How this list was built

PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08
did not return a complete extractable Warren County-filtered result list.
This pilot is therefore a **partial pilot**, anchored on:

- PA 211 search results retrievable by text fetch (Overnight Shelters and
  Permanent Housing searches, which surface Warren County items among
  statewide results);
- the shelterlistings.org Warren County, PA page;
- the Warren/Forest Counties resource hub and the Inglis / PHFA Warren County
  Quick Start housing guide (Feb 2025, updated Jun 2026);
- the Forest and Warren Counties FY 2024-2025 Human Services Plan (pa.gov);
- organisations' own websites.

Each item below records the directory claim as found, and the question that
was open before checking. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered
to Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.

## Gaps noted while building the list (not listings)

- **No dedicated youth emergency shelter was found in Warren County, PA.**
  The nearest verified youth shelter programme is Perseus House in Erie
  (court-ordered residential, not a walk-in youth shelter) — see out-of-scope
  list below.
- **No dedicated winter / warming shelter (Code Blue) was found for Warren
  County, PA** in this check. The Faith Inn is the county's year-round
  emergency shelter. Warming-centre results returned for "Warren" were
  Warren, Michigan; Warren County, Virginia; and Warren County, New Jersey.
- **No separate family shelter** beyond the Faith Inn (families) and A Safe
  Place (survivors with children) was found in-county.
- Forest County (served jointly with Warren by the EOC and A Safe Place) has
  no shelter of its own in this pilot; Forest County residents are served
  through the Warren County sites above.
