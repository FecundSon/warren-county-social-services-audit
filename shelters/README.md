# Warren County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other
directory. No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 18 shelter listings for Warren County, PA (a rural
county in northwestern Pennsylvania; county seat Warren, PA), plus 12
out-of-county exclusions, checked against the organisations' own websites
and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement
directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open shelters, and it
should not be used as one without checking with the shelter or PA 211
first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.

Warren County's in-county shelter stock is genuinely small: one general
emergency shelter (the Faith Inn), one domestic-violence shelter in a
confidential location (A Safe Place), one veterans transitional-housing
site (Allegheny Valley Veterans Center, Clarendon), and referral and
assistance programmes that place people in shelter or housing rather than
running a shelter building. No dedicated youth shelter and no dedicated
winter/warming shelter were found in the county.

## Headline corrections (from the full audit)
1. **Erie VAMC Homeless Care Program — the Warren listing points at the
   wrong kind of building.** PA 211 lists it at 3 Farm Colony Drive,
   Warren, PA 16365. VA's own site shows that address is the Warren
   County VA Clinic, a mental-health outpatient clinic — not a shelter.
   The Homeless Care Team is based at Erie VA Medical Center, 135 East
   38th Street, Erie, PA. Homeless veterans should be directed to the VA
   National Call Center for Homeless Veterans, **1-877-424-3838**
   (24 hours), not to the Warren clinic as a shelter location.
2. **Faith Inn — income limit is out of date in directory text.** Older
   directory text says eligibility is household income 0–30% of area
   median income. The EOC's own programmes page now says **income below
   80% of area median income**, with in-person application at the housing
   office at the Faith Inn, 225 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA
   16365, or 814-726-2400 ext. 3358.
3. **Salvation Army Warren — "Emergency Shelter" needs qualifying.**
   The Corps' own site confirms 311 Beech Street, Warren, PA 16365,
   814-723-8950, Mon–Fri 9am–2pm, but no on-site shelter is confirmed on
   its own current site. The county's Human Services Plan describes the
   role as a **one-night motel voucher for families in emergency
   situations**. Directories should say motel-voucher referral via the
   Corps office, not an on-site shelter, and office hours are not shelter
   hours.
4. **Veterans Leadership Program SSVF — one PA 211 record understates
   its coverage.** VLP's own site lists Warren County in its SSVF service
   area (intake 844-VLP-VETS), but PA 211's Johnstown-office record for
   the same programme omits Warren County. The Johnstown record should be
   corrected to match the programme's own stated coverage.
5. **A Safe Place — confirmed, and its location must stay confidential.**
   The domestic-violence shelter is confirmed on its own site: 24-hour
   hotline **814-726-1030 or 1-800-338-3460**, 24-hour access to shelter
   or other safe housing, up to 12 people at a time, stays up to 30 days,
   all services free. Its shelter is in a confidential location and no
   address for it appears in this audit.
6. **Four "Warren County" shelters are other states' Warren Counties.**
   HARTS (Front Royal, VA), Family Promise of Warren County (New Jersey /
   Ohio), Turning Point Advocacy Services (Warrenton, VA) and WAIT House
   (Glens Falls, NY) do not belong in a Warren County, PA listing.
7. **No youth shelter in the county.** No dedicated emergency shelter for
   unaccompanied youth was found in Warren County, PA. The nearest
   verified youth shelter programme is Perseus House in Erie (Erie
   County), which is residential care for court-ordered adolescents, not
   a walk-in youth shelter.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No shelter, PA
  211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state
  a fact, that item is marked CLARIFIED in the full report — it is a gap
  in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be published as
  fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact.** Only intake
  phones and intake processes are reported.
- **One shelter in this audit is a domestic-violence safe house in a
  confidential location (A Safe Place).** It is counted (1 item) and its
  public hotline numbers and public admin office are given, because the
  organisation itself publishes those. Its shelter address is not
  published anywhere in these files and must never be added to a public
  copy.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete
  extractable Warren County-filtered result list. The pilot is therefore
  a **partial pilot** anchored on PA 211 items retrievable by text fetch
  (Faith Inn; Erie VAMC Homeless Care Program; VLP SSVF records) plus
  the shelterlistings.org Warren County page, the Inglis/PHFA Warren
  County housing guide, the Forest and Warren Counties Human Services
  Plan, and organisations' own sites. A live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org filtered to Warren County is still outstanding
  before any send to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Every in-county item was checked
  against its exact address, not its ZIP code.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the shelters themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
