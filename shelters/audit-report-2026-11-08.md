# Warren County, PA — Shelter Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, Pennsylvania (county seat Warren, PA; towns include
Youngsville, Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North
Warren, Starbrick, Clarendon). Rural county in northwestern Pennsylvania.
**Category:** Shelters (emergency, family, youth, veterans).
**Directory checked against:** statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org / pa211.org).
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to statewide
PA 211, to any shelter, or to any other directory. No organisation was
contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin. Keith is NOT a local resident of Warren County.
This audit was prepared with assistance from his AI assistant, Muse.

## Method and limits — please read before using this

- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No shelter, no
  directory, and no one else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over third-party
  directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state a fact, that
  item is marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED in the table — a gap in public web
  evidence, not a finding, and it must not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as a closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a shelter is currently operating.
- Office hours from a church/office/centre page are not shelter hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- **Bed availability is never published as a static fact in this audit.**
  Only the intake phone and intake process are reported.
- **One shelter in this audit is a domestic-violence safe house in a
  confidential location (item 6, A Safe Place). Its shelter address is NOT
  published anywhere in these files.** It is counted (1 confidential-location
  item) and its public 24-hour hotline numbers and public admin office are
  given, because the organisation itself publishes those. The shelter
  location must never be added to a public copy of this audit.
- **Limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 did not return a complete extractable
  Warren County-filtered result list. This is therefore a **partial pilot**,
  anchored on PA 211 items retrievable by text fetch (Faith Inn; Erie VAMC
  Homeless Care Program; VLP SSVF records) plus the shelterlistings.org
  Warren County page, the Inglis/PHFA Warren County housing guide, the
  Forest and Warren Counties Human Services Plan, and organisations' own
  sites. It is NOT a complete county directory, and a live-browser check of
  search.pa211.org filtered to Warren County is still outstanding before any
  send to PA 211.
- Warren County is a small rural county. The in-county shelter stock is
  genuinely small: one general emergency shelter (Faith Inn), one
  domestic-violence shelter (confidential location), one veterans
  transitional-housing site (Clarendon), and referral/assistance programmes
  that place people in shelter or housing rather than running a shelter
  building. The pilot therefore includes the county's housing-adjacent
  programmes and the near neighbours most likely to be confused with
  Warren County listings, so the exclusions are on the record too.
- ZIP code does not settle county. Every in-county item below was checked
  against its exact address, not its ZIP code.

## Headline findings

1. **Erie VAMC Homeless Care Program — the Warren listing points at the
   wrong kind of building.** PA 211 lists the programme at 3 Farm Colony
   Drive, Warren, PA 16365, (866) 682-3250. VA's own site shows that
   address is the **Warren County VA Clinic, a mental-health outpatient
   clinic** — not a shelter and not the Homeless Care Team's base. The
   Homeless Care Team is based at Erie VA Medical Center, 135 East 38th
   Street, Erie, PA. A homeless veteran following the PA 211 listing to
   Warren would arrive at an outpatient clinic. The listing should direct
   veterans to the VA National Call Center for Homeless Veterans,
   **1-877-424-3838** (24 hours), or to the Erie Homeless Care Team, and
   should not present 3 Farm Colony Drive as a shelter location. The Warren
   clinic itself (item 9) is a legitimate referral point, but it is a
   clinic, not a shelter, and it shares the same address — the two listings
   are a duplicate address for two different things.
2. **Faith Inn — income limit is out of date in directory text.** Older
   directory text (shelterlistings.org, from the Emergency Solutions Grants
   description) says eligibility is household income 0–30% of area median
   income. The EOC's own programmes page now says **income below 80% of
   area median income**, with in-person application at the housing office
   at the Faith Inn, 225 Pennsylvania Avenue West, Warren, PA 16365, or by
   calling 814-726-2400 ext. 3358. Directories should use the own-site 80%
   figure.
3. **Salvation Army Warren — "Emergency Shelter" needs qualifying.** The
   Corps' own site confirms the address (311 Beech Street, Warren, PA
   16365), phone (814-723-8950) and office hours (Mon–Fri 9am–2pm), but its
   services list did not state emergency shelter on the page fetched, and
   there is no on-site shelter confirmed on the Corps' own current site.
   The county's Human Services Plan describes the Salvation Army's
   emergency-shelter role as a **one-night motel voucher for families in
   emergency situations**. Directories listing "Emergency Shelter" at
   311 Beech Street should say motel-voucher referral via the Corps office
   during office hours — not an on-site shelter, and office hours are not
   shelter hours.
4. **Veterans Leadership Program SSVF — one PA 211 record understates its
   coverage.** VLP's own site lists Warren County in its SSVF service area
   (intake 844-VLP-VETS). PA 211's Johnstown-office record for the same
   programme lists a service area that omits Warren County. The
   Pittsburgh/Erie PA 211 records include Warren. The Johnstown record
   should be corrected to match the programme's own stated coverage.
5. **A Safe Place — confirmed, and its location must stay confidential.**
   The domestic-violence shelter is confirmed on its own site: 24-hour
   hotline **814-726-1030 or 1-800-338-3460**, 24-hour access to shelter or
   other safe housing, up to 12 people at a time, stays up to 30 days, all
   services free. Its shelter is in a **confidential location** and no
   address for it appears in this audit. Its public admin office (210 North
   Drive, Suite C, on the Warren State Hospital campus, Warren, PA) is
   published by the organisation itself and is not the shelter.
6. **Four "Warren County" shelters are other states' Warren Counties.**
   HARTS (Front Royal, VA), Family Promise of Warren County (New Jersey /
   Ohio), Turning Point Advocacy Services (Warrenton, VA) and WAIT House
   (Glens Falls, NY) all serve a different Warren County. None of them
   belongs in a Warren County, PA listing, and HARTS in particular — a
   winter shelter that is "the only winter shelter in the county" in its
   own county — is easy to mistake for a Warren County, PA winter shelter.
   Warren County, PA has **no dedicated winter/warming shelter** found in
   this check.
7. **No youth shelter in the county.** No dedicated emergency shelter for
   unaccompanied youth was found in Warren County, PA. The nearest verified
   youth shelter programme is Perseus House in Erie (Erie County), which is
   residential care for court-ordered adolescents, not a walk-in youth
   shelter. This is a gap in provision, not a listing error, and it is
   recorded here so it is not mistaken for an oversight.

## What this report does NOT claim

- It does **not** claim to be a complete directory of Warren County shelters.
  PA 211's Warren County-filtered list could not be pulled in full by text
  fetch, and a live-browser check is still outstanding. Items may exist in
  PA 211 that are not in this pilot.
- It does **not** claim any organisation is closed or defunct. Nothing in
  this audit is a closure finding.
- It does **not** publish bed availability, waitlist position, or current
  occupancy for any shelter. Only intake phones and intake processes are
  reported.
- It does **not** publish the location of the A Safe Place shelter, or of
  any other confidential domestic-violence safe house. One item in this
  audit (item 6) is counted only.
- It does **not** claim the Faith Inn's 30-day stay limit from the EOC's own
  site — that detail comes from PA 211 and secondary listings, and should
  be re-confirmed with the EOC before it is sent anywhere.
- It does **not** claim the Coordinated Entry hours (Mon–Fri 7:30am–4pm,
  Option 5) from the EOC's own site — that detail comes from a Continuum of
  Care flyer and the Inglis guide.
- It does **not** present the Salvation Army Warren's emergency shelter as
  confirmed: the motel-voucher description comes from the county's
  2015-16 Human Services Plan, which is old, and the Corps' own current
  site did not confirm it.
- It does **not** claim a youth shelter or a winter/warming shelter exists in
  Warren County — none was found, and that absence is a gap in provision,
  not proof that no informal arrangement exists.
- A January 2025 Point-in-Time count of 112 people experiencing
  homelessness in Warren County was cited in a local social-media post
  during this check. That figure is **not verified** from an official
  source in this audit and is recorded here only as context, not as a
  finding.
