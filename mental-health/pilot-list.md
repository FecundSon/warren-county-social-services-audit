# Warren County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services: Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren, PA; other towns: Youngsville,
Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick,
Clarendon). **Date built:** 2026-11-08. **Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI
assistance from Muse. **The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.**

## Sources for this list
- Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org) — Suicide Prevention Hotlines taxonomy
  page, read 2026-11-08. PA 211 search pages are JavaScript-driven; the text
  fetch returned a statewide (not Warren-filtered) result list, so this is a
  **partial pilot**, not a complete Warren County directory extract.
- Warren & Forest Family Resource Hub
  (fwhs-resource-hub.family-navigator.workers.dev) — a publication of the
  Mental Health Family Navigator at Forest Warren Human Services; its
  "Mental Health & Substance Use" section lists 15 providers.
- Warren County government Crisis Lines page (warrencountypa.gov/1371/Crisis-Lines).
- Pennsylvania Department of Human Services county crisis list and county
  MH/ID offices page (pa.gov).
- Organisations' own websites (see audit report for URLs).

   Crisis Lines page; PA 211 pinned result): call or text 988, or chat

   verified against a VA page in this check (see audit report).

   Family Resource Hub, crisis help section): 800-932-0313.
