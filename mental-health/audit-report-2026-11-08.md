# Warren County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, PA. **Category:** Mental health / crisis services.
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08. **Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Pennsylvania Department of Human
Services, or any provider. No organisation was contacted.**

**Auditor:** Keith Conklin, with AI assistance from Muse. **The auditor is NOT
a local resident of Warren County.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 25 mental health / crisis listings for Warren County,
PA, plus 5 out-of-county exclusions (30 pilot entries total), checked against
the organisations' own websites and other public listings. It is a curation
audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list of
open services, and it should not be used as one without checking with the
provider or PA 211 first.

This audit checks **contact information only** — names, addresses, phone
numbers, hours, who is served, and appointment vs walk-in. It does not
rewrite, summarise, or invent crisis or treatment advice.

## Headline findings (strong enough to send, with own-site evidence)
1. **PA 211's "Forest Warren Crisis Line" shows the wrong address and phone.**
   PA 211 lists it as 214 South 7th Avenue, Suite 110, Clarion, PA 16214,
   (814) 226-1080. That is Center for Community Resources' (CCR) Clarion
   administrative office, not a Warren County crisis location. CCR's own
   Forest and Warren Counties page
   (https://www.ccrinfo.org/forest-and-warren-counties) gives the crisis
   number as **Call 844-757-3224, Text 63288**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a
   week, for telephone, chat and text crisis services **and** mobile crisis
   services. Warren County government's own Crisis Lines page
   (https://www.warrencountypa.gov/1371/Crisis-Lines) and Forest Warren
   Human Services' own Family Resource Hub give the same number,
   1-844-757-3224 / text 63288. PA 211 should list the crisis number, not
   the Clarion office number, for this entry.
2. **The older PA Department of Human Services crisis list still gives
   Forest-Warren Human Services numbers as Warren County's crisis numbers —
   814-726-8413, and 800-406-1255 after hours.** Current county, FWHS and
   CCR publications all point to CCR 1-844-757-3224 as the local crisis
   line. FWHS's daytime number **814-726-2100** remains the county MH/ID
   office number (285 Hospital Drive, Warren), not the crisis line. PA 211
   and PA DHS listings that still show the FWHS numbers as the crisis
   numbers should be updated to CCR 1-844-757-3224 — with FWHS/CCR asked to
   confirm whether the old numbers still route anywhere before they are
   removed (see pre-send re-check list).
3. **Beacon Light Warren Outpatient Clinic phone — apparent digit
   transposition in the provider's own notice.** Directories and Beacon
   Light's own October 15, 2026 advertisement give **(814) 723-1832**,
   1885 Market Street, Warren. Beacon Light's own October 29, 2026
   "IMPORTANT NOTICE" about its Warren Anger Management Group prints
   **(814) 723-1382**. One of the two is a transposition (1832 vs 1382).
   This audit does not assert which is correct; Beacon Light should be
   asked to confirm. Flagged here because a wrong digit in a clinic number
   is exactly the error this audit exists to catch.
4. **Family Services of Warren County — counselling address not stated on
   its own site; two addresses circulate.** PA 211 lists 589 Hospital
   Drive, Suite E, Warren, PA 16365. Warren Gives lists 119 Market St,
   Warren, PA 16365 as FSWC's location. FSWC's own site (fswc.org) states
   the phone (814) 723-1330, hours (Mon & Thu 8am–6pm; Tue & Wed 8am–8pm;
   Fri 8am–2pm), ages served (5 and up) and services, but no street address
   in the pages read. PA 211 should confirm the counselling-site address
   with FSWC rather than publish either secondary address from this audit.
5. **Kinzua Counseling & Psychological Services — two different phones in
   circulation.** The FWHS Family Resource Hub and the Psychology Today
   practitioner profile give **814-343-9043**; Birdeye and other directory
   listings give **814-728-6074**. Address agrees (315 Second Avenue,
   Suite 403, Warren, PA 16365). No standalone practice website was found
   to settle it. Needs confirmation with the practice before any send.
6. **Like a River Counseling — announced move.** The practice's own
   Facebook post (2026-11-03) announces a new location effective
   **December 1, 2026: 3 Locust St, Warren**, phone unchanged 814-688-2671.
   Listings showing 438 Pennsylvania Avenue West will go stale on that
   date; re-check after December 1.

## What this report does NOT claim
- **It is not a complete directory of Warren County mental health services.**
  PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and the text fetch on
  2026-11-08 returned a statewide, not Warren-filtered, result list. This
  audit is a partial pilot anchored on retrievable PA 211 items, the FWHS
  Family Resource Hub, Warren County government's crisis page, and
  organisations' own sites. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org
  filtered to Warren County is still outstanding before any send.
- **No organisation is called closed or defunct.** Deerfield Behavioral
  Health's Warren status is UNVERIFIED because of conflicting evidence
  (own site lists Warren; a JHS newsletter reports its Warren outpatient
  clinics closed May 15) — that is a gap, not a closure finding. Nothing
  here should be read as "this service no longer exists."
- **No walk-in crisis centre with a public Warren County address was found.**
  Crisis help in Warren County, as published, is phone/text/chat plus
  mobile response (CCR comes to the caller), plus 988, plus hospital
  emergency care. Do not present any address in this audit as a walk-in
  crisis centre.
- **A live website does not prove a service is currently operating**, and
  hours taken from business-listing/place data are not confirmed hours.
- **No crisis or treatment advice is given in this audit.** Only contact
  information was checked.
- **Language access:** no provider's own site in this check stated
  language-access or interpretation services, except CCBH (Spanish member
  line 1-866-229-3187; TTY via 711, request 1-833-545-9191) and the PA
  Support & Referral Helpline (TTY 724-631-5600). Absence from this audit
  does not mean a provider offers no language access — it means it was not
  stated on the pages read.
- **ZIP code does not settle county.** Several listings found in this check
  share Warren-area ZIP codes but sit in other counties (e.g. Clarion,
  McKean, Erie) or another state (New York, New Jersey). County was checked
  by exact address, not ZIP.
- **Private practices are included only where publicly listed as services**
  (FWHS hub, directories, or the practice's own public pages). No
  clinician's personal phone is published here; where only a practice
  number was publicly listed, only that number is given.
