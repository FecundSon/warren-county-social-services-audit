# Warren County, PA — Mental Health / Crisis Services Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, the Pennsylvania Department of Human
Services, or any provider. No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 25 mental health / crisis listings for Warren County,
PA, plus 5 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the organisations' own
websites and other public listings. It is a curation audit, not a replacement
directory: it does not claim to be a current list of open services, and it
should not be used as one without checking with the provider or PA 211
first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.

This audit checks **contact information only** — names, addresses, phone
numbers, hours, who is served, and appointment vs walk-in. It does not
rewrite, summarise, or invent crisis or treatment advice.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an organisation's own site did not state
  an address or hours, that item is marked CLARIFIED or UNVERIFIED in the
  full report — a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and it must
  not be published as fact.
- **No organisation is called closed or defunct in this audit.** Deerfield
  Behavioral Health's Warren status is UNVERIFIED because its own site
  still lists a Warren address/phone while a Journey Health System
  newsletter reports its Warren outpatient clinics closed May 15 — that
  conflict is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a service is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a business-listing page are not a provider's own hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- 988 is the national Suicide & Crisis Lifeline; the county crisis line
  (CCR 1-844-757-3224) is local. One is not a replacement for the other,
  and this audit does not replace one with the other.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven,
  and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned a statewide, not Warren-filtered,
  result list. The pilot is therefore anchored on retrievable PA 211 items
  plus the FWHS Family Resource Hub, Warren County government's crisis
  page, and organisations' own sites, each item labelled with its claim
  source. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered to Warren
  County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county: several listings sharing Warren-area
  ZIP codes sit in Clarion, McKean or Erie County, or in New York or New
  Jersey, and are excluded as out of scope.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211, the Pennsylvania Department of Human
  Services, or the providers themselves are welcome — that is the point of
  staging it here first.
