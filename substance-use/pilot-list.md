# Warren County, PA — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery: Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; other towns: Youngsville,
Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick,
Clarendon). **Category:** Substance use treatment / recovery.
**Date compiled:** 2026-11-08. **Status: not sent yet. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation was
contacted.**

## What this list is
The candidate list this audit was checked against. Each entry records the
directory claim as found (name, address, phone, services, hours, who served)
and the source that claim came from, before any checking against the
organisation's own website. The audit report
(`audit-report-2026-11-08.md`) records what the check found.

**The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.** This audit was
prepared with AI assistance (Muse). ZIP code does NOT settle county —
exact-address geocoding does; every address below was checked against its
town, and out-of-county items are listed separately at the end.

## How the list was built — read this first
PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven. A text fetch of PA 211's
statewide "Substance Use Counseling" taxonomy page on 2026-11-08 returned
a statewide, unfiltered list (about 25 named listings on the page fetched)
with **no Warren County entry in it** — the nearest were in Erie County
(LECOM detox, 5515 Peach Street, Erie; Stairways Behavioral Health,
2919 State Street, Erie). No Warren County-filtered PA 211 extract could be
pulled by text fetch. One PA 211 item WAS retrieved directly: Beacon Light —
Youngsville Treatment Center, from PA 211's Children's/Adolescent
Residential Treatment Facilities taxonomy page.

This pilot is therefore anchored on that one PA 211 item plus the
Forest Warren Human Services family resource hub (a provider-neutral local
list), SAMHSA-derived directories, the PA Department of Drug and Alcohol
Programs / PA government sources, and the organisations' own websites.
**It is a partial pilot, NOT a complete county directory.** A live-browser
check of search.pa211.org filtered to Warren County is still outstanding
before any send to PA 211.
