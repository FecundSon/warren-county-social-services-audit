# Warren County, PA — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211 or any organisation. No organisation
was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 27 substance use treatment / recovery listings for
Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; other towns Youngsville, Sheffield,
Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Bear Lake, North Warren, Starbrick,
Clarendon), plus 3 out-of-county exclusions, checked against the
organisations' own websites and other public sources. It is a curation
audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim to be a current list
of open services, and it should not be used as one without checking with
the provider or PA 211 first. The auditor is NOT a local resident of
Warren County.

   "2 Pennsylvania Avenue West"; the hospital is at **2 Crescent Park

7. **Erie VA Medical Center — Warren CBOC is veterans-only**, and its

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state an address or
  hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED with the gap stated
  — it is a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and must not be
  published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page, and a single third-party "closed" label (Primary
  Health Network Warren), are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a programme is currently
  operating.
- **Bed/placement availability is never reported.** Only the intake
  phone and process are recorded.
- **No treatment advice is given and no programme is claimed to work.**
  This audit checks contact information only.
- Hours from a church/office/centre page are not service hours, and are
  flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- Meeting days and times for AA, NA and Al-Anon change frequently and
  were checked only against third-party meeting aggregators, not against
  the fellowships' official meeting locators. All twelve meeting items
  are UNVERIFIED.
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 of PA 211's statewide
  "Substance Use Counseling" taxonomy page returned a statewide,
  unfiltered list (about 25 named listings) with no Warren County entry
  in it. No Warren County-filtered PA 211 extract could be pulled by
  text fetch. One PA 211 item was retrieved directly (Beacon Light
  Youngsville, from PA 211's Children's/Adolescent Residential Treatment
  Facilities page). The pilot is therefore anchored on that PA 211 item
  plus the Forest Warren Human Services family resource hub,
  SAMHSA-derived directories, PA government sources, and the
  organisations' own sites. **This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete
  county directory.** A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered
  to Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- Two name traps are recorded in the pilot list: BrightView's Warren
  Addiction Treatment Center is in Warren, Ohio, not Warren, PA; and
  "102 Warren Street, Tunkhannock" on PA 211's statewide page is a
  street name in Wyoming County.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.

## Counts
27 in-scope listings: 4 CONFIRMED, 1 CORRECTED, 8 CLARIFIED,
14 UNVERIFIED, plus 3 OUT OF SCOPE exclusions (30 pilot entries total).
The 14 UNVERIFIED are the 12 AA/NA/Al-Anon meeting items (aggregator
times only, not checked against official locators), the Primary Health
Network Warren clinic (status unresolved), and the Achievement Center of
LECOM Health Warren office (children's behavioral health; no substance
use service stated). No residential/inpatient substance use rehab (other
than hospital detox) and no recovery housing for men were found in-county
in this pilot — a gap in the pilot's coverage, not proof that none exists.
