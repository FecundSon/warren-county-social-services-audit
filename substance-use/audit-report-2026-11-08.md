# Warren County, PA — Substance Use Treatment / Recovery Listing Audit

**County:** Warren County, PA. **Category:** Substance use treatment /
recovery. **Date checked:** 2026-11-08.
**Method:** public web search and page-text fetch only. No organisation,
PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
**Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit has been sent to
statewide PA 211 or any organisation.**

**The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.** This audit was
prepared with AI assistance (Muse) for Keith Conklin, who is also not a
local resident of Warren County.

## Headline findings
Only the corrections strong enough to send (own-site or official-government
evidence, material to someone trying to use the service).

1. **Deerfield Centers for Addiction Treatment (DCAT), Warren — address
   is wrong in directories.** Directories still list 514 West Third
   Avenue, Warren, PA 16365. Deerfield Behavioral Health's own site
   lists its Warren office at **1885 Market Street, Warren, PA 16365,
   phone 814-723-5545**, and current SAMHSA-derived data now shows the
   same 1885 Market Street address. The phone number is unchanged.
   PA 211 should list 1885 Market Street and drop 514 West Third Avenue
   (or confirm with the provider whether the Third Avenue site is still
   in use).
2. **Family Services of Warren County — two addresses circulate.**
   119 Market Street, Warren, PA 16365 (Google place data, rehab.com,
   startyourrecovery.org, and a PA DDAP visit flyer for the "Warren
   County Recovery Center") versus 589 Hospital Drive, Suite E, Warren,
   PA 16365 (SAMHSA-derived aggregators recovered.org and
   rehabangels.org). The organisation's own site (fswc.org, fetched
   2026-11-08) states no street address on its home page, so this could
   not be settled from the own site. Phone (814) 723-1330 is consistent
   everywhere. PA 211 should confirm which address is current.
   Hours ARE confirmed from the own site: Monday and Thursday
   8 a.m.–6 p.m., Tuesday and Wednesday 8 a.m.–8 p.m., Friday
   8 a.m.–2 p.m. (rehab.com's "Mon–Wed 8 a.m.–8 p.m." is wrong for
   Monday.)
3. **Crossroads Treatment Centers of Warren — clinic hours, and a
   Tue/Thu swap in aggregators.** Own site
   (crossroadstreatmentcenters.com, fetched 2026-11-08): 412 Market
   Street, Warren, PA 16365; call centre 855-694-8288, open 24/7/365;
   clinic hours Tuesday 1:00–5:00 p.m., Thursday 10:00 a.m.–2:00 p.m.,
   Friday 8:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m., Sunday 8:30 a.m.–2:00 p.m. Some
   aggregator place data swaps Tuesday and Thursday. The own-site hours
   should be used.
4. **Beacon Light — Youngsville Treatment Center is NOT a drug rehab.**
   Drug-rehab aggregators file 585 East Main Street, Youngsville,
   (814) 584-1124 as an inpatient drug rehab / residential treatment
   centre for children. PA 211's own taxonomy page lists it under
   Children's/Adolescent Residential Treatment Facilities: treatment for
   children and adolescents with mental health and behavioral needs
   requiring residential treatment. It should not appear in substance
   use treatment lists.
5. **Warren State Hospital — wrong phone number in an aggregator, and
   it is not a substance use entry point.** One aggregator
   (drugrehabcenters.org) lists Warren State Hospital's phone as
   814-584-1124 — that is Beacon Light Youngsville's number. The
   hospital's own government page (pa.gov) gives 33 Main Drive, North
   Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-5500. It is a public psychiatric hospital
   for long-term mental health treatment, including co-occurring
   disorders; admission comes only from a community inpatient
   psychiatric hospitalisation. It should not be listed as a place to
   seek substance use treatment.
6. **Warren General Hospital detox — address variant to drop.**
   Directories agree on 2 Crescent Park West, Warren, PA 16365, main
   (814) 723-3300, Behavioral Health (814) 723-4973, detox information
   (814) 723-3300 ext. 1985. One aggregator gives "2 Pennsylvania
   Avenue West", which is not the hospital's address. The hospital's own
   site (wgh.org) confirms a Detoxification Unit exists (visitor guide).
   Detox admissions begin through the Emergency Care Center; the
   programme is voluntary, 4A level of care, adults 18+, and directories
   state it does not provide methadone or Suboxone/Subutex treatment —
   that last point rests on directory text, not on an own-site programme
   page fetched in this check.
7. **Erie VA Medical Center — Warren CBOC is veterans-only, and its
   substance use care runs through Erie.** The VA's own material says
   certain Behavioral Health services are available at the Warren CBOC
   in person or by telehealth, and to self-refer/enrol call the Erie
   VAMC Behavioral Health Clinic at 814-860-2038 or 1-800-274-8387.
   The CBOC address (3 Farm Colony Drive, Warren) and phone
   (866-682-3250) come from aggregators, not from va.gov in this check.
   A listing that presents the Warren CBOC as a general-public
   substance use clinic is wrong.
8. **Primary Health Network — Warren Community Health Center: status
   unresolved, do NOT publish as closed.** A Google place entry labels
   it "Permanently Closed", while the PA Department of Health facility
   directory lists Primary Health Network, 1885 Market Street, Suite B,
   Warren, PA 16365, as an open Federally Qualified Health Center, and
   other directories still show hours. It is a primary-care clinic, not
   a substance use treatment programme. Current status is UNVERIFIED;
   no closure is claimed in this audit.

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No provider,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- An organisation's own website is treated as authoritative over
  third-party directories. Where an own site did not state an address or
  hours, that item is marked UNVERIFIED or CLARIFIED with the gap stated
  — a gap in public web evidence, not a finding, and it must not be
  published as fact.
- **No organisation is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed
  or missing page, and a single third-party "closed" label (Primary
  Health Network Warren), are reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a programme is currently
  operating.
- **Bed/placement availability is never reported.** Only the intake
  phone and process are recorded. Availability changes constantly.
- **No treatment advice is given and no programme is claimed to work.**
  This audit checks contact information only.
- Hours from a church/office/centre page are not service hours, and are
  flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible.
- Meeting days and times for AA, NA and Al-Anon change frequently and
  were checked only against third-party meeting aggregators, not against
  the fellowships' official meeting locators. Every meeting item is
  UNVERIFIED.
- **One limit, stated plainly:** PA 211's search pages are
  JavaScript-driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 of PA 211's
  statewide "Substance Use Counseling" taxonomy page returned a
  statewide, unfiltered list (about 25 named listings) with no Warren
  County entry in it. No Warren County-filtered PA 211 extract could be
  pulled by text fetch. One PA 211 item was retrieved directly (Beacon
  Light Youngsville, from PA 211's Children's/Adolescent Residential
  Treatment Facilities page). The pilot is therefore anchored on that
  PA 211 item plus the Forest Warren Human Services family resource hub,
  SAMHSA-derived directories, PA government sources, and the
  organisations' own sites. **This is a partial pilot, NOT a complete
  county directory.** A live-browser check of search.pa211.org filtered
  to Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- Two name traps surfaced and are recorded in the pilot list:
  BrightView's Warren Addiction Treatment Center is in Warren, Ohio,
  not Warren, PA; and "102 Warren Street, Tunkhannock" on PA 211's
  statewide page is a street name in Wyoming County.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the organisations themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.

  programme has capacity. Only intake phones and processes are recorded.

  works or is effective.

  All twelve meeting items are UNVERIFIED against official locators.

  No residential/inpatient substance use rehab (other than hospital

  exists.

- A live website or listing does not by itself prove a programme is
  currently operating.
