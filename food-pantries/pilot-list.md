# Warren County, PA — Food Pantries: Pilot List

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; towns: Warren,
Youngsville, Pittsfield, Tidioute, Sugar Grove, Russell, Sheffield,
Clarendon, North Warren, Starbrick, Bear Lake)
**Category:** Food pantries / food distributions
**Directory:** Statewide PA 211 (search.pa211.org) is the directory
for Pennsylvania. PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-driven and a
text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned only the topic landing page — no
complete Warren County-filtered list could be extracted. This pilot
is therefore anchored on retrievable items, not on a complete PA 211
pull.
**Pilot built:** 2026-11-08, before checking.
**Auditor:** NOT a local resident of Warren County.
**Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## Claim sources for this list
- **Flyer A** — "FOOD RESOURCES FOR WARREN COUNTY," shared by Warren
  Area High School and Douglas Hearn on Facebook, October 2025.
  Lists pantries by area (Warren; Youngsville/Tidioute; Sugar Grove/
  Russell; Sheffield) with addresses and hours.
- **Flyer B** — "Food pantries, distributions, and community meals,"
  shared by Rob Shaffer on Facebook, October 2025. Sixteen items
  with address, phone, and hours; includes some out-of-county items.
- **Flyer C** — First Methodist Church of Warren resource flyer
  (firstmwarren.org PDF, 2025): pantry/distribution details plus
  2025 community-meal calendar.
- **foodpantries.org** — Warren, PA / Warren County, PA pages
  (foodpantries.org/ci/pa-warren, /co/pa-warren).
- **Forest Warren Human Services resource hub**
  (fwhs-resource-hub.family-navigator.workers.dev) — "Food & Meals,"
  18 listings for Warren and Forest Counties.
- **PA DHS** — Senior Food Box (CSFP) county lead agency directory,
  updated December 2025 (pa.gov).
- **Second Harvest Food Bank of Northwest Pennsylvania**
  (nwpafoodbank.org) — the regional food bank serving Warren County
  among 11 counties.
