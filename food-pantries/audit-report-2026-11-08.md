# Warren County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit Report

**County:** Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; towns covered:
Warren, Youngsville, Pittsfield, Tidioute, Sugar Grove, Russell,
Sheffield)
**Category:** Food pantries / food distributions
**Date checked:** 2026-11-08
**Method:** Public web search and page-text fetch only. Each listing
was checked against the organisation's own website (including its own
Facebook page). Own site beats directory; directory beats aggregator.
No organisation, pantry, or directory was contacted.
**Status: NOT SENT YET.** Nothing in this report has been sent to
statewide PA 211, Second Harvest Food Bank of Northwest Pennsylvania,
or any pantry. The auditor is NOT a local resident of Warren County.
**Prepared with AI assistance (Muse).**

## What this report does NOT claim
- It does not claim to be a complete list of Warren County food
  pantries. PA 211's search pages could not be pulled as a complete
  Warren County-filtered list (see Method and limits), so this is a
  partial pilot anchored on retrievable items.
- It does not claim any pantry is closed or defunct. UNVERIFIED
  means "no own-site evidence found on 2026-11-08," not "closed."
- It does not claim a pantry is currently operating just because its
  website is live.
- It does not present church office hours, thrift-shop hours, or
  centre hours as pantry hours.
- It does not present a monthly distribution as a weekly pantry, a
  meal as a grocery pantry, or a programme for a specific group
  (military households, seniors 60+) as a general pantry.
- It does not give eligibility or visit-frequency rules beyond what
  the organisation's own site stated on the check date.
- Seasonal dates (e.g. Sugar Grove's December 19, 2026 Saturday
  distribution) are as published on the check date and will go stale.

## Method and limits
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted.
- Own site (including the organisation's own Facebook page) beats
  directory; directory beats aggregator.
- **PA 211 limit, stated plainly:** search.pa211.org is
  JavaScript-driven. A text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned only the
  topic landing page; no Warren County-filtered result list could be
  extracted. The pilot is therefore anchored on two community "Food
  Resources for Warren County" flyers (Warren Area High School /
  Douglas Hearn, October 2025; Rob Shaffer, October 2025), a First
  Methodist Church of Warren resource flyer (2025), foodpantries.org
  Warren County pages, the Forest Warren Human Services resource hub
  (fwhs-resource-hub.family-navigator.workers.dev), and the
  organisations' own sites. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org
  for Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county here: "near Warren" lists mix in
  Erie, Forest, and Clarion County (PA) and Chautauqua County (NY)
  pantries. County was settled by exact address, not ZIP.
- Some schedules rest on dated sources and are flagged as such:
