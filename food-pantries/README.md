# Warren County, PA — Food Pantry Listing Audit

**Checked: 2026-11-08. Status: staged here only. Nothing in this audit
has been sent to statewide PA 211, Second Harvest Food Bank of
Northwest Pennsylvania, or any pantry. No organisation was contacted.**

## What this is
A small, dated audit of 20 food pantry / food distribution listings
for Warren County, PA (county seat Warren; other towns Youngsville,
Sheffield, Tidioute, Russell, Sugar Grove, Pittsfield, Clarendon,
North Warren, Starbrick, Bear Lake), plus 7 out-of-county exclusions,
checked against the pantries' own websites and other public listings.
It is a curation audit, not a replacement directory: it does not claim
to be a current list of open pantries, and it should not be used as
one without checking with the pantry or PA 211 first. The auditor is
NOT a local resident of Warren County.

2. **Produce Express at First Lutheran Church, 109 W 3rd Ave, Warren**
   — a **monthly** distribution, **3rd Thursday of the month at 2pm**,

3. **Calvary Baptist Church, 445 Conewango Ave, Warren** — a

   (**4th Thursday of the month at 3pm**; a Second Harvest event was

   walk-in pantry.
4. **Salvation Army Warren Worship & Service Center, 311 Beech St,
   Warren, (814) 723-8950** — the centre's general hours (Mon–Fri
   9am–2pm) are **not pantry hours**. The pantry is by appointment,
   plus a monthly Fresh Pantry on the 4th Wednesday (10am–2pm and
   5–7pm). The soup kitchen (lunch Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 12–1pm, Wednesday
   dinner 4:30–5:30pm) is a separate meal service, not the pantry.

   Non-perishable food boxes are available by request through the

6. **Sheffield: "Sheffield Area Food Pantry" and "Sheffield
   Ecumenical Food Pantry" are one pantry** at **18 Leather St,
   Sheffield, PA 16347** — do not list them twice. Its current
   distribution schedule was not verified on any own site.
7. **Youngsville: "2nd Street Food Pantry" and "Youngsville
   Ministerium Food Pantry" are one pantry** at **18 2nd St,
   Youngsville** (at Youngsville New Life Church) — do not list them

   **501 Pennsylvania Ave E, Warren, PA 16365, (814) 723-9440**

## Method and limits — please read before using this
- Public web search and page-text fetch only, 2026-11-08. No pantry,
  PA 211, or anyone else was contacted for this audit.
- A pantry's own website (including its own Facebook page) is treated
  as authoritative over third-party directories. Where a pantry's own
  site did not state an address or hours, that item is marked
  UNVERIFIED in the full report — it is a gap in public web evidence,
  not a finding, and must not be published as fact.
- **No pantry is called defunct or closed in this audit.** A failed or
  missing page is reported as unverified, never as closure.
- A live website does not by itself prove a pantry is currently
  operating.
- Hours from a church/office/thrift page are not pantry hours, and
  are flagged as such wherever that confusion was possible (Salvation
  Army, Sugar Grove FMC, Grace UMC, First Presbyterian).
- Monthly distributions are not weekly pantries, and meals are not
  grocery pantries; both distinctions are flagged wherever they
  applied (First Lutheran, Calvary Baptist, Military Share, Pittsfield
  Otterbein, First Presbyterian, St. Joseph's).
- One limit, stated plainly: PA 211's search pages are JavaScript-
  driven, and a text fetch on 2026-11-08 returned only the topic
  landing page — no complete extractable Warren County result list.
  The pilot is therefore anchored on two community "Food Resources
  for Warren County" flyers (Warren Area High School / Douglas Hearn,
  October 2025; Rob Shaffer, October 2025), a First Methodist Church
  of Warren resource flyer (2025), foodpantries.org Warren County
  pages, and the organisations' own sites, each item labelled with
  its claim source. A live-browser check of search.pa211.org for
  Warren County is still outstanding before any send to PA 211.
- ZIP code does not settle county in this area: several pantries that
  appear on "near Warren" lists are in fact in Erie County (Corry),
  Forest County (Tionesta), Clarion County (Marienville, Knox), or
  Chautauqua County, NY (Frewsburg). They are listed as OUT OF SCOPE
  exclusions, not as Warren County pantries.
- This audit was prepared with AI assistance. Corrections, challenges,
  and updates from statewide PA 211 or the pantries themselves are
  welcome — that is the point of staging it here first.
